## Business & Why It Exists

Booking Holdings (NASDAQ: BKNG) is the world's largest OTA by gross bookings, operating six platforms: **Booking.com** (global accommodations leader, ~80%+ of revenue), **Priceline** (U.S. discount travel), **Agoda** (Asia-Pacific), **Kayak** (metasearch), **OpenTable** (restaurant reservations), and **Rentalcars.com** (car rentals). Booking.com (founded Amsterdam 1996; acquired by Priceline for **$133M in 2005** — one of the great mega-cap M&A returns) solved an impossible coordination problem: matching 3M+ properties across 220+ countries with hundreds of millions of travelers, online first and at the greatest scale. The strategic vision is the **Connected Trip** — booking and managing every trip element (accommodation, flight, car, attraction, restaurant) in one flow, which diversifies away single-vertical dependence and builds a fulfillment moat an AI travel agent cannot replicate. ~90% of revenue is non-U.S. (Europe-heavy via Booking.com B.V., Netherlands).
