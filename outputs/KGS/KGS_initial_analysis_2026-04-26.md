## Business & Why It Exists

Kodiak owns and operates large-horsepower compression units deployed at well sites, gathering systems, and midstream facilities to move natural gas across the Permian and U.S. production regions. Fee-based model: take-or-pay, multi-year contracts with investment-grade midstream/E&P customers — economics resemble a utility more than a cyclical oilfield-services name (~9% of HP is month-to-month per 10-K). Two segments: **Contract Services** (~90% rev — company/customer-owned compression + gas treating under fixed multi-year contracts) and **Other Services** (~10% — station construction, overhaul, freight, crane). The new **Kodiak Power Solutions** vertical (post-Apr 1, 2026) adds behind-the-meter gas power gen.

**Three transformative events 2024–2026:**

| Event | Date | Size | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| CSI Compressco acquisition | Apr 1, 2024 | ~$870M | +580K HP; became undisputed #1 U.S.; fully integrated by YE2024 |
| Mexico operations exit | Sep 30, 2025 | $34.3M proceeds | $33.3M loss; pure-U.S. focus; 97.7% utilization is pure-U.S. |
| DPS → Kodiak Power Solutions | Apr 1, 2026 | ~$675M ($575M cash + 2.4M sh) | +395 MW distributed power gen; new AI-data-center vertical |

**EQT AB exit**: PE sponsor (formerly ~43.1% of shares) fully exited the register by Dec 2, 2025 — all secondary-offering overhang removed.

**Narrow** — scale + large-HP specialization + asset specificity + take-or-pay structure in a 3-player U.S. oligopoly.

**Moat sources**: scale #1 (4.35M HP ≈ ~24% of ~17–18M HP U.S. market) · large-HP depth (970 avg HP/unit, deepest >1,500 HP fleet for Permian gas-lift → premium pricing) · asset specificity (custom large compressors, 12–18 mo replacement lead; switching rare/costly) · take-or-pay with IG counterparties (top customers Williams, Energy Transfer, Targa) · 97.7% utilization vs industry ~85–90% (near-zero idle → no renewal leverage for customers).

## §7 — Industry Macro

**TAM**: U.S. installed contract compression ~17–18M HP; KGS 4.35M = ~24%. **Demand drivers**: (1) Permian associated-gas growth (GOR rises as wells age → structurally bullish per barrel); (2) LNG build-out ~14→~25 Bcf/d by 2028; (3) AI data-center power (behind-the-meter gas is fastest-deployable large-scale power; AI load +40%/yr per hyperscaler capex); (4) pipeline-to-power (gas as baseload/backup for renewables). **Regulatory**: methane rules favor modern large-HP fleet (tailwind); pro-LNG admin but Jan 2024 pause shows bipartisan volatility; tariffs a new equipment-cost risk; **OBBBA full expensing (Jul 4, 2025)** lowered FY25 effective tax rate — structural FCF tailwind. **Cyclicality**: 2014–16 oil collapse drove util. to ~80%; today's 97.7% leaves near-zero slack (worse downside starting point), but take-or-pay mitigates near-term realization (only ~9% HP month-to-month).
