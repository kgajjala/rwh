## Business & Competitive Overview

PepsiCo was assembled through three defining transactions: the 1965 Pepsi-Cola/Frito-Lay merger, the 1998 Tropicana acquisition, and the 2001 Quaker Oats/Gatorade merger. FY2025 net revenue was $93.9B. The portfolio spans Lay's, Doritos, Cheetos, Tostitos, Ruffles (Frito-Lay); Quaker, Gatorade; and Pepsi, Mountain Dew, Aquafina, Bubly, Rockstar (beverages).

**Moat: Wide.** PepsiCo's direct-store-delivery (DSD) network of 300,000+ routes and the "Power of One" snacks+beverages bundle give it shelf-space and promotional leverage no single-category peer can match. Snacks/foods are ≈55–57% of revenue but carry far higher margins (FLNA ≈22–26% operating margin) than beverages (PBNA ≈4–9%), so snacks drive the majority of profit.

**Leadership**: Ramon Laguarta (CEO/Chairman since October 2018); Steve Schmitt became CFO November 10, 2025 (recruited from Walmart U.S.). Elliott Investment Management disclosed a ≈$4B stake in 2025 and engaged constructively; PepsiCo's shareholder-value plan (productivity acceleration, board refreshment, portfolio focus) was publicly endorsed by Elliott.
