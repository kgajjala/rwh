## Business & Why It Exists

Rocket Companies (NYSE: RKT) is the largest U.S. mortgage originator (~25% non-bank share; $130.4B 2025 closed volume) and largest U.S. servicer (~$2.7T MSR portfolio post-Mr.-Cooper). After the 2025 Redfin acquisition it runs the only fully integrated end-to-end U.S. homeownership platform: home search (Redfin, ~100M MAU) → origination (Rocket Mortgage) → servicing (Mr. Cooper, Rocket) → personal-finance AI (Rocket Money). The combined entity is no longer a pure-cycle originator but an originate-to-service-to-recapture super-platform — search funnel feeds origination, the $2.7T servicing book feeds AI-driven recapture, and Rocket Logic strips ~50% of 2020 headcount out of the cost base. The bull path needs Redfin attach to ramp and Cooper synergies to land; the bear path is sustained 7.5%+ rates re-running the 2022–2023 trough against a now-goodwill-heavy balance sheet. **Operating brands**: Rocket Mortgage, Rocket Money, Rocket Homes, Redfin, Bay Equity, Mr. Cooper.

## §7 — Industry Macro

**Lock-in effect**: 85–90% of outstanding mortgages carry sub-5% rates; with 30-yr at 6.5–7.0%, lock-in suppresses purchase *and* refi simultaneously — the trough Rocket has navigated. **Unlock scenario**: any sustained move toward 5.75–6.25% unlocks purchase volume, triggers a refi wave from 7%+ borrowers, and lifts origination at high GOS (partly offset by lower MSR fair-value gains). Even 50–75bps of relief = industry-wide $300–500B+ volume increase, of which RKT takes ~8–10%.

| Scenario | Industry origination | RKT share *[Est]* | RKT volume |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2023 trough | ~$1.3T | ~6% | ~$80B |
| 2024–25 base | ~$1.6–1.8T | ~7–8% | ~$101–130B |
| Normal market | ~$2.0–2.5T | ~8–10% | ~$160–250B |
| Refi-boom (2020 analog) | ~$3.0–4.0T | ~10–12% | ~$300–480B |

Non-banks now control ~65%+ of origination vs. banks; RKT's purchase share alone went 3.8%→5.5% in 12 months — structural shift + RKT-specific gain converging. **Regulatory**: CFPB active under any administration, with the integrated-platform model novel and the [RESPA class-action](https://www.law360.com) (Jan 2026, *[link pending]*) the leading-indicator test; watch FHFA loan-limit / conforming-threshold / FHA-premium changes; state predatory-lending rules not currently binding.
