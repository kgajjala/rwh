## Business & Why It Exists

The Charles Schwab Corporation (NYSE: SCHW) is the largest U.S. retail brokerage by client assets — **$11.8T across ~46.5M accounts** (Q1 2026). Founded by Chuck Schwab in 1971 on the insight that the true brokerage product is the *platform and custody* (not commissions), Schwab built the discount-brokerage industry after May Day 1975 deregulation. Five decades later the founding insight reached its endpoint — *zero* commissions — and Schwab now monetizes float on client cash (NII) plus value-added services (advisory, asset management, banking, RIA custody). Revenue ≈ Client Assets × Monetization Rate: $11.8T × ~0.20% net interest ≈ $23.6B. **2025 transformation**: TD Ameritrade integration declared complete YE2025 (final 17M+ accounts converted May 2024); cash-sorting structurally resolved (high-cost debt ~$97B peak 2023 → $5.1B); sweep balances rebuilt organically (+$13.5B Q3 2025, +$8B Q1 2026 sequential). **Rick Wurster** became CEO Jan 1, 2025; Walt Bettinger transitioned to Co-Chairman alongside founder Charles Schwab.

**How the moat differs**: (1) **Scale + integrated bank** is unique — only public broker with an in-house bank funding the NII engine, the structural reason NIM sustains 2.85%+ vs. wirehouse-bundled peers at sub-2% (NIM 2.90% FY25 vs. IBKR ~3.5% smaller balance sheet). (2) **RIA custody ~$4T** is the most defensible asset — thousands of advisor practices with quantifiably high ACATS/operational switching costs; net-asset gathering stayed positive every quarter through the cash-sorting scare. (3) **Post-TDA active-trader retention** — thinkorswim is best-in-class, the gap pre-TDA vs. Robinhood/IBKR; Q1'26 DARTs record 9.9M (+34%) directly attributable. (4) **Brand + scale on advice/retirement** create the Boomer-rollover gravity well; Fidelity is the only peer with comparable trust + scale ($158B Q1 NNA despite soft tape). **Tail risk**: Fidelity could run negative-margin promotions (not happening); Robinhood cohort-aging over 5–10 years; PFOF SEC review (<10% revenue exposed, politically visible).
