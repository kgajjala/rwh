## Business & Why It Exists

Tesla is the leading western pure-play EV maker + a vertically integrated energy-storage business + an emerging AI/autonomy/robotics platform, run as **three economic engines** with very different return profiles. **Engine 1 — Auto (~80% rev)**: Model 3/Y/S/X, Cybertruck, imminent **Cybercab** (purpose-built robotaxi) + entry-level "next-gen affordable" platform ("Model 2"); FY25 deliveries ~1.78M (flat), Q1'26 358,023; auto GM ex-credits mid-teens, Q1'26 GAAP total GM **21.1%** (best in 18 months). **Engine 2 — Energy (~12% rev, 25–30% GM)**: Megapack utility-scale + Powerwall residential; **46.7 GWh deployed FY25** (record), Q1'26 8.8 GWh (−38% seq, lumpy); Powerwall network drives **89,000+ VPP events across 1M+ installations** — network layer emerging. **Engine 3 — AI/autonomy/robotics (optionality)**: FSD (Supervised) on monthly-sub only since Q4'25; Robotaxi live Austin (June 2025), Dallas + Houston unsupervised (April 18, 2026); Cybercab production "officially begun"; Optimus Gen 3 SOP target before YE 2026; $2B xAI investment. The ~$1.41T cap can't be justified by Engine 1 alone (auto peers trade 5–10× P/E → ~$70–120/sh); the remaining ~$256/sh embeds Engine 2 + Engine 3 optionality.

## §3 — Competitive Moat & Landscape

## §7 — Industry Macro

**EV TAM**: ~17M units 2025 → ~30M by 2030; Tesla ~10% (BYD ~17%), share flat-to-falling — growth must come from TAM expansion + monetization layers (FSD/Robotaxi/Optimus/energy). **Autonomy TAM**: McKinsey/ARK $5–10T over 15 yrs if L4 works at scale, winner-take-most (top 2–3 capture majority); Waymo operating, Tesla a cost-advantaged fast-follower. **Energy-storage TAM**: growing 50%+/yr on grid decarbonization + intermittency; Tesla Megapack category leader (~25%). **Humanoid TAM**: speculative ($25T+ Musk "if every human had one"); realistically low-volume industrial/logistics by 2027, consumer 2030+; Tesla's 1M/yr ultimate capacity is at the high end of credible peer projections. **Regulatory**: Trump-admin EV credits cut in the 2025 reconciliation bill, partly offset by lower AV-deployment friction (NHTSA/state permissive); EU pushes EV mandates but the Chinese-import tariff structure is the key variable for European share recovery. **Macro sensitivity**: TSLA trades like risk-on tech despite being an automaker — any 2026 risk-off hits multiple compression and sentiment together.
