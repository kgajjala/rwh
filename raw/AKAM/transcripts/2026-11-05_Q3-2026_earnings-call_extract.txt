LABELLED EXTRACT of the Q3 2026 earnings call, Nov 5, 2026 4:30pm ET. Source read in full: https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/11/06/akamai-akam-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/ (Motley Fool, LLM-assisted transcript; may contain errors). Cross-check summaries: Benzinga, MarketBeat, TradingKey (not re-read). Quotes below are verbatim from the Fool transcript.

Leighton (prepared): "we signed an $11.6 billion commitment over 7 years with Anthropic to support their CPU workloads... This brings the total of multiyear commits for CIS signed this year to more than $14 billion. The total includes an earlier large contract with Anthropic, 2 large deals with leading AI companies, and numerous multimillion-dollar deals with major enterprises."
Leighton: "Two of the first 3 large deals are now generating revenue... the third will begin generating revenue later this quarter. We did encounter some modest supply chain and third-party data center delays with 2 of the deals and so our revenue outlook for Q4 will be less than expected."
Leighton: "we believe that the large deals we've signed this year will contribute an aggregate adjusted EBITDA margin of about 75% and a non-GAAP operating margin of approximately 30% when they're fully ramped." "about $22 million of revenue per megawatt of contracted power." "well over 90% [of servers] are CPUs." "the largest cloud contracts we signed this year guarantee revenue for these CPUs for 7 years." "we don't even offer bare metal."
McGowan (prepared): slide posted on IR site: full $2.2B annual revenue from the $14B contracts; "expected after-tax internal rate of return of approximately 13% and expected non-GAAP average annual adjusted return on invested capital of approximately 15%"; revenue ramps from Q4 2026, fully ramped by end of 2028. First 3 major contracts = $2.8B total contract value. ~$30M revenue shifts from Q4'26 to 2027. $11.6B deal: all space/power secured, ~70% of hardware secured incl. $1.7B memory etc. in Q4'26; pricing adjustment mechanism for unordered hardware. $7B 2028 assumes no revenue from the $9B Anthropic option.
McGowan: no repurchases Q3; ~$565M remaining authorization; cash+securities ~$4.2B; "fund our CIS expansion and broader growth investments through 2028 as well as address our 2027 convertible note maturity, primarily from our existing cash balance and expected operating cash flows, which may be supplemented by measured incremental financing activities". "meaningful increase in our operating cash flow beginning in 2028".
McGowan: Q4 capex $2.445-2.645B; FY2026 capex $3.462-3.662B. Q4 cash gross margin ~70%, adj EBITDA margin ~38%. FY2026 CIS growth 40-42% cc; security high single digits; delivery down mid-single digits. Company will not announce every large CIS deal.
Q&A: 2027 revenue phasing 25-30% first half / 70-75% second half (McGowan to DiFucci). Margin bottoms in 1H27 "maybe a point or so lower" than now, 1-2 pts non-cash straight-line colo pressure (to Ader). Anthropic IRR is the floor: "these are the lowest prices you're going to see" (to Chatterjee/Levine/Kessinger); smaller deals "significantly higher" IRR. Investment grade: "our goal would be to maintain investment grade as long as we certainly can" (to Morgan Stanley). James Fish (Piper): "We've kind of missed compute or only been in line on virtually every quarter that you've had this business." McGowan declined to litigate the past. McGowan: do not assume Anthropic option tranches; "there's an incentive for them as our stock goes higher with the warrants that are attached". Delivery: stable, pricing holding better, "down a point or 2 or 3". Security adds ~$12-13M sequential per quarter. LayerX acquisition closed (AI-use data-leak security).
Leighton on market: "the market got pretty well spooked as you know, and we got dragged down with the market... unfairly so" (reference to post-midterms AI-infrastructure scrutiny).
