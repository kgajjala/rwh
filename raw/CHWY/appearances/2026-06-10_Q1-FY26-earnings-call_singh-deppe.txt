- FY2026 net sales guide CUT to $13.40–13.55B (+6.3–7.5%) from $13.60–13.75B; adj EBITDA margin held 6.6–6.8%.
- Singh: "The consumer pet environment has become incrementally more challenged"; "We are seeing a modest level of incremental pressure on premiumization and product attach rates amongst our current customer base, resulting in what we view as a short-term NSPAC headwind."
- Active customers 21.5M (+3.6%); net adds now expected at LOW end of 150–250K/quarter range. NSPAC $597 (+4.6% normalized). Autoship 84.4%.
- Vet Care: 18 CVC + 29 Modern Animal = 47 clinics; ~60 by year-end 2026. CVC year-one NSPAC ~$900; ~40% of clinic customers new to Chewy. Modern Animal ~$70M FY26 sales, EBITDA-dollar neutral in 2026.
- Buybacks ~$200M in Q1. $600M Term Loan B for "strategic growth and shareholder returns". Leverage ceiling: net leverage below 2x adj EBITDA (Deppe).
- Competition: Singh "we don't see indicators that suggest a meaningful change in the competitive environment"; promotions "active but ... rational". On Amazon same-day grocery: "No real comments... we keep competitors in the rearview mirror."
- 10% adj EBITDA margin reaffirmed "over time", no date.
