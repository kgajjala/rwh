Goldman Sachs Global Consumer and Retail Conference — fireside chat, September 14, 2026, 2:50 pm ET
Speaker: Sumit Singh (CEO)
Synthesis of: https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/chewy-at-goldman-sachs-conference-health-and-ai-drive-next-phase-93CH-4900307
Also: https://finvaulta.com/research/goldman-sachs/chewy-global-consumer-and-retail-conference-key-takeaways-2026-09-14 [not fetched]

- Gross margin ~18% at IPO → ~30% today. Chewy Health ~$4B of the ~$9B incremental revenue over six years (~44%).
- Pet population: "adoptions and relinquishments are running at about parity"; dog population "slowed down a little bit". Frames weakness as normalization, not structural decline.
- Claims Chewy captures "70 cents of every dollar moving online in the pet pharmacy and products space" (off-script claim, no source given).
- Vet clinic unit economics (new disclosure vs filings): ~$3.5M revenue/clinic + ~$0.8M attach-back to Chewy.com ≈ $4.3M; breakeven ~20 months; 20–30% better than industry averages; 4 in 10 clinic customers new to Chewy; vet one-year retention high-80s to low-90s %.
- Chewy+: "a new improved version is in development"; tests of pricing/Autoship expansion: "given the pressure generally on attach, our expectations were not as strong as what we had forecasted."
- AI: ~$50M savings in 2027; "Cai" assistant; partnerships with Google/Gemini.
- Capital: "not about to enter an investment cycle"; can "self-fund a majority of these investments". Priorities: (1) reinvest in clinics, AI, Autoship; (2) "opportunistic mergers and acquisitions in health"; (3) return capital.
- No pricing recovery baked into 2027. No 10% margin date given. International "not a primary priority". BC Partners not discussed.
