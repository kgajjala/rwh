NOTES (not a verbatim copy) on the Chipotle Q3 2026 earnings call, October 28, 2026
Source read in full in the browser: https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/29/chipotle-cmg-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/ (Motley Fool; second source not retrieved, see gaps in the changelog)
Speakers: Scott Boatwright (CEO), Adam Rymer (CFO)

- Boatwright: "we are a category leader"; Recipe for Growth "still in the early stages"; high-efficiency equipment package (HEEP) in over 1,700 restaurants, ~2,000 by year-end; throughput "Mise en Place and Aces in their places" in fewer than half of restaurants; Taste of Food scores up nearly 1 point.
- Rewards: over 24 million active members; loyalty share of sales over 33%, a record. Pollo Asado and Chili Lime Chips launched Aug 31, attachment rate "in the low 20% range". Smoked Brisket launches the week after the call. Three new markets in the quarter: Mexico, Saudi Arabia, South Korea.
- Rymer: industry headwinds beginning in mid-July slowed momentum; promotions used to re-engage guests; trends normalized by late August; September improved; "October trends have softened, consistent with the broader industry." Boatwright: September comps +3.8% with very little promotion; after a pullback in early October "we have since regained positive transactions."
- Pricing: ~2.7% in Q3, ~3% in Q4, ~2% for 2026. Inflation in 2027 expected low to mid single digits.
- Q4 guide (Rymer): comps "around a plus 2%" with a 3-4% exit rate; no 2027 comp guide until the next call. Cost of sales ~30.5% (Brisket LTO), labor ~26%, other operating costs ~16% (marketing high-3% range), restaurant-level margin "just under 22%", ~150 bps below last year (half of it a 70 bps prior-year gift-card breakage benefit, ~30 bps the Brisket marketing shift). G&A ~$189M non-GAAP. D&A low 3% of sales.
- Q3 margin walk: cost of sales 29.5% (-50 bps y/y: price and tariff relief offset beef/freight), labor 25.7% (+50 bps: volume, wages, promotional labor, discrete insurance costs), other operating 16.0% (+100 bps: marketing +40 bps, delivery mix, utilities, maintenance).
- Capital: ~$1B cash, restricted cash and investments, no debt. Year to date repurchases $1.4B at an average $34.32; ~$1.6B authorized remaining. "During the quarter, there was a period where we were unable to repurchase our stock" (blackout); will return after the blackout lifts.
- Europe: company-operated comps in the "low double-digit range" in Q3 (high single digit in Q2).
- Evercore's David Palmer asked about October and whether comps "continue to ramp maybe 3% plus"; he described the perception that October comps were down.
