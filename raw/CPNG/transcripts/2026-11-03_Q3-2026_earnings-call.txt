Bom Kim, Founder and CEO:
"Our expectation in returning next year to Product Commerce margins approximating pre-incident levels is unchanged."
"We do not believe any of these items are structural. Some are near-term investments we have chosen to make, while others are temporary headwinds."
"The soft spot in reported growth this quarter remains a small cohort of members who left after the data incident and have not yet returned."
"As we lap the affected periods next year, the non-returning cohort drops out of the year-over-year comp, and our reported growth converges with the growth of the underlying customer base."
"We believe Q3 represents the low point for our Product Commerce adjusted EBITDA margins."
"The pace of improvement that you asked about, that may vary quarter-to-quarter, given seasonal factors and the speed at which we work through each of these items."
"When we're at this early stage and we're growing as fast as we are, a lot of the infrastructure investments that are required for the level of service that we're trying to provide, they do involve a lot of lead time. So we do build ahead of demand."
"As we continue to execute with discipline, we expect Taiwan to be a meaningful contributor to consolidated cash flow in the years to come."
Taiwan: WOW membership up more than 100% YoY and 14% QoQ; Dawn Delivery reaches over 50% of the population.

Gaurav Anand, CFO:
"For Q4, we expect consolidated constant currency revenue growth of 13%-14%."
"For consolidated adjusted EBITDA, we expect the margin to increase 50-150 basis points quarter-over-quarter in Q4, reflecting continued underlying progress in Product Commerce, as well as reduced levels of marketing spend."
"We continue to expect Product Commerce adjusted EBITDA margins to return by mid-next year to levels approximating those we generated prior to the data incident."
"For Developing Offerings, we previously guided to full-year losses this year of $950 million-$1 billion. We are now increasing the top end of that range by $50 million-$100 million. This update is primarily driven by our Taiwan build-out."
"On cash flows, on the trailing 12-month basis, we generated operating cash flow of $845 million, and free cash flow was negative $409 million. The year-over-year declines reflect the lower profitability we have discussed this year, increases in capital expenditure, cash outlays relating to the fire, and elevated levels of tax payments."
Chuseok timing: about 1 pt drag on CC revenue growth and about 40 bps on adjusted EBITDA margin. PC gross margin ex fire 29.5%, -260 bps YoY and -100 bps QoQ; PC adjusted EBITDA margin down 420 bps YoY and 50 bps QoQ.

Q&A (from the transcript summary and page text; paraphrased): Goldman (Cha) asked for the margin cadence and whether DO losses peak this year or next: Kim gave no quarterly path and did not say whether losses have peaked. JPMorgan (Yang): Anand said Korea and Japan Eats run around break-even; did not answer the 2027 funding question; full 2027 DO guidance comes with Q4. Morgan Stanley (Park): marketing is for returning members, "not a competitive response". No question or answer on the KFTC, the PIPC fine, borrowing or fire insurance.
