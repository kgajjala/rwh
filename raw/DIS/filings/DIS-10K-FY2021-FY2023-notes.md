## FY2021 10-K
**Filing**: FY ended October 2, 2021. Filed November 2021 (accession 0001744489-21-000220).
**URL** (unfetched): https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001744489/000174448921000220/dis-20211002.htm

Key figures:
- **Total revenue**: $67.4 billion (recovery year from COVID-19 impact; +3%, or +$2.0B, YoY).
- **Diluted EPS (continuing operations)**: $1.11, vs. a loss of $(1.57) in FY2020.
- **Net income**: $2.0 billion (improved by $4.9 billion YoY).
- **Capital expenditures**: $3.6 billion.
- **Total borrowings**: $54,406 million ($54.4 billion) — the peak of Disney's debt load across the FY2021–FY2025 window, reflecting the 21st Century Fox acquisition debt plus COVID-era liquidity borrowing.
- **Total segment operating income**: not directly cited; **derived** at ~$7.7 billion by working backward from the FY2022 press release's stated "+56%, or +$4.4 billion, to $12.1 billion" YoY framing ($12.1B − $4.4B ≈ $7.7B). Flagged as derived, not primary-sourced.
- Segment structure in this filing: two-segment basis (Disney Media and Entertainment Distribution / Disney Parks, Experiences and Products) — pre-dates the FY2024 three-segment (Entertainment/Sports/Experiences) reorganization. Direct comparison of segment-level figures to FY2024/FY2025 requires normalization; not attempted in this pass.
- Source citations: [Last10k FY2021 10-K index](https://last10k.com/sec-filings/dis/0001744489-21-000220.htm), WebSearch aggregation of FY2021 press release figures.

## FY2022 10-K
**Filing**: FY ended October 1, 2022. Filed November 2022 (accession 0001744489-22-000213).
**URL** (unfetched): https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001744489/000174448922000213/dis-20221001.htm

Key figures:
- **Total revenue**: $82,722 million ($82.7 billion) — calculated in the filing as segment revenues of $83,745 million less a $1,023 million reduction tied to a content-license early-termination item. Source: [WDWNT FY2022 revenue coverage](https://wdwnt.com/2022/11/the-walt-disney-company-reports-82-billion-in-revenue-for-2022-fiscal-year/), cross-referenced against FY2021's $67,418 million base.
- **Diluted EPS (continuing operations)**: $1.75, up from $1.11 in FY2021.
- **Total segment operating income**: $12.1 billion, +56% (+$4.4 billion) YoY — increase driven by higher operating income at Disney Parks, Experiences and Products, partially offset by lower operating income at Disney Media and Entertainment Distribution (i.e., streaming losses continued to weigh on the media segment even as Parks fully reopened post-COVID).
- **Capital expenditures**: $4.943 billion.
- **Total borrowings**: $48,369 million ($48.4 billion), down from $54.4B in FY2021.
- This was the year of Disney's peak annual DTC/streaming operating loss (~$4 billion), embedded within the Media and Entertainment Distribution segment figure above.
- Source citations: [Q4 FY2022 earnings release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448922000192/fy2022_q4xprxex991.htm) (via WebSearch extraction).

## FY2023 10-K
**Filing**: FY ended September 30, 2023. Filed November 2023 (accession 0001744489-23-000216).
**URL** (unfetched): https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001744489/000174448923000216/dis-20230930.htm

Key figures:
- **Total revenue**: $88.9 billion, +7% YoY.
- **Diluted EPS (continuing operations, GAAP)**: $1.29, down from $1.75 in FY2022 (impacted by restructuring/impairment charges).
- **Adjusted EPS (excluding certain items)**: $3.76, up from $3.53 in FY2022.
- **Total segment operating income**: $12,863 million ($12.9 billion), up from $12,121 million ($12.1 billion) in FY2022.
- **Capital expenditures**: $4.969 billion (essentially flat vs. FY2022's $4.943 billion).
- **Interest expense**: $1,973 million, up from $1,549 million in FY2022 — reflects both higher debt-servicing costs and the higher-rate environment.
- **Total borrowings**: $46,431 million ($46.4 billion) — current portion $4,330 million; long-term portion $42,101 million.
- This was the fiscal year of the proxy contest with Trian Partners / Nelson Peltz (activist campaign contesting board seats) — culminated in Disney's 2023 annual meeting; relevant to the multi-year governance narrative even though it predates the current CEO-succession cycle. Also the year Bob Iger returned as CEO (Nov 2022) after Bob Chapek's departure, meaning FY2023 was Iger's first full fiscal year back.
- Also: **dividend reinstated** in this fiscal year — $0.30/share, first dividend declaration since the COVID-19 suspension, paid January 10, 2024 (record date December 11, 2023).
- Source citations: [Q4 FY2023 earnings release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448923000213/fy2023_q4xprxex991.htm), [FY2023 Annual Report PDF](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/app/uploads/2024/02/2023-Annual-Report.pdf) (both cited via WebSearch extraction, not directly fetched).
