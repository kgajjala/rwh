## Item 1 (Business) — Segment Structure
FY2024 was the **first full fiscal year** under the current three-segment structure (Entertainment / Sports / Experiences), which Disney adopted starting in FY2024 reporting (previously reported as Disney Media and Entertainment Distribution (DMED) + Disney Parks, Experiences and Products (DPEP), with ESPN/Sports broken out as its own segment beginning FY2024). This is itself a structural discontinuity worth noting for any multi-year trend analysis — FY2021–FY2023 figures are reported/restated under the old two-segment (or transitional) structure in most secondary sources; care needed reconciling to the new three-segment basis.

## Item 7 (MD&A) — FY2024 Full-Year Results
Source: [Q4/FY2024 earnings release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448924000275/fy2024_q4xprxex991.htm) via WebSearch extraction; [Monexa FY2024 financials summary](https://www.monexa.ai/blog/the-walt-disney-company-fy2024-financials-cash-flo-DIS-2025-08-14).

- **Total revenue**: $91.4 billion, up 3% from $88.9 billion in FY2023 (source consistent across FY2025 press release prior-year comps and dedicated FY2024 searches).
- **Total segment operating income**: $15.6 billion (implied from FY2025 press release YoY framing: "$17.6 billion from $15.6 billion in the prior year").
- **Operating income (GAAP, consolidated)**: ~$11.91 billion, +32.5% YoY (per [Monexa](https://www.monexa.ai/blog/the-walt-disney-company-fy2024-financials-cash-flo-DIS-2025-08-14) — note this consolidated GAAP figure differs from the "total segment operating income" figure above, which excludes corporate/eliminations/restructuring; both are legitimate different metrics, flagged to avoid conflation).
- **Diluted EPS (GAAP)**: $2.72, more than doubled from $1.29 in FY2023.
- **Adjusted EPS**: $4.97 (basis for the FY2025 press release's "+19% to $5.93" comparison).
- **Free cash flow**: ~$8.6 billion, nearly doubled YoY.
- **Capital expenditures**: $5.4 billion, up from $5.0 billion in FY2023 — cited driver: higher spend on cruise ship fleet expansion and new Experiences attractions, partially offset by lower Corporate facilities spend.
- **Total borrowings**: $45,815 million ($45.8 billion) at FY2024 year-end (per FY2025 10-K prior-year comparative, cross-checked — supersedes an initially-seen but likely-erroneous "$49.52B" figure from one lower-quality aggregator; the $45.8B figure is corroborated directly by the FY2025 filing's own prior-year column and is treated as authoritative).

### Direct-to-Consumer (Streaming) — FY2024 Milestone
- Disney's DTC streaming segment (Disney+, Hulu, ESPN+) achieved **operating income of $321 million in Q4 FY2024**, a major turnaround milestone.
- Full-year FY2024 DTC swung to profitability after **FY2022 peak annual streaming loss of ~$4 billion** (source: [StreamTV Insider](https://www.streamtvinsider.com/video/disney-dtc-streaming-profit-surges-321m), general streaming-turnaround coverage). Company had guided that Disney+ specifically would achieve profitability in fiscal 2024 — guidance that was met.

## Capital Allocation Context (FY2024)
- Dividend: Disney paid $0.45/share in July 2024 (semi-annual installment), following the initial $0.30/share reinstatement dividend paid January 2024 (record date Dec 11, 2023) — first dividend since the COVID-19 suspension. Source: [Hollywood Reporter dividend reinstatement coverage](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/business/business-news/disney-brings-back-dividend-after-covid-suspension-1235704225/), [Bitget Disney dividend history](https://www.bitget.com/wiki/disney-stock-dividend).
- December 2024 installment: $0.50/share — bringing FY2024 total annual dividend to $0.75/share combined (per FY2025 notes cross-reference: "33% increase from the $0.75 total paid in 2024" implies FY2024 full-year total was $0.75/share).
