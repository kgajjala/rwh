## CEO SUCCESSION — HEADLINE MATERIAL EVENT (highest priority finding)

This is the single most consequential governance event in Disney's recent history and should be the anchor of §4 (Management & Leadership) in the wiki page.

**Timeline**:
- Bob Iger's contract had been extended to December 31, 2026 to provide leadership stability while the board finalized succession (source: [mlq.ai succession timeline coverage](https://mlq.ai/news/bob-iger-announces-disney-ceo-succession-timeline-for-early-2026/)).
- **February 3, 2026**: Disney's Board of Directors, in a unanimous vote, elected **Josh D'Amaro** (then-Chairman of Disney Experiences) as the next CEO of The Walt Disney Company, effective at the Annual Meeting on March 18, 2026. Source: [Official Disney announcement](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/disney-ceo-announcement/), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/03/disney-ceo-josh-damaro-successor-bob-iger.html), [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/news/josh-damaro-disney-ceo-bob-iger-1236650641/).
- **March 18, 2026**: D'Amaro officially became Disney's **ninth CEO** in the company's ~103-year history, coinciding with the 2026 Annual Shareholders Meeting. Source: [CNBC "new chapter" coverage](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/18/disney-josh-damaro-new-chapter.html).
- **Bob Iger's post-CEO role**: transitioned to Senior Advisor and remains a member of the Board of Directors through the end of 2026 (per his extended contract terms).
- **Board chairmanship**: James P. Gorman (previously/currently Executive Chairman of Morgan Stanley) replaced Mark Parker as Disney's Board Chairman in January 2026, and led the CEO succession planning committee. Source: [Hollywood Reporter succession-strategy coverage](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/business/business-news/disney-bob-iger-succession-james-gorman-1236048452/).

**Other internal candidates considered** (per pre-announcement reporting): Dana Walden, Josh D'Amaro, Jimmy Pitaro, Alan Bergman. D'Amaro was selected.

**Concurrent leadership move**: **Dana Walden** (previously Co-Chairman of Disney Entertainment) was named **President and Chief Creative Officer** of The Walt Disney Company, effective March 18, 2026, overseeing film and television businesses and reporting directly to D'Amaro. Source: cross-referenced via [8-K Item 5.02 filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001744489/000174448926000022/fy2026_q2x8kxneoxex991.htm) (unfetched, cited via WebSearch summary).

**D'Amaro background**: 28-year Disney veteran; architect of the largest global expansion in Disney Experiences history; led the Experiences segment (which posted $36 billion in FY2025 annual revenue and employs ~185,000 Cast Members/employees worldwide) to record financial and guest-satisfaction results prior to becoming CEO. Source: [Official Disney CEO announcement page](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/disney-ceo-announcement/).

**D'Amaro compensation package** (as new CEO): Source: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/02/03/disney-ceo-bob-iger-josh-damaro-45-million/), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/disney-ceo-josh-d-amaro-221724293.html).
- Base salary: $2.5 million.
- Target annual bonus: 250% of base = $6.25 million.
- Annual long-term equity award target: $26.25 million.
- Total first-year target package (including a one-time award, assuming full payout): ~$44.7 million (one source states "~$45 million"; another states a headline "$38.4M" figure for the recurring/target annual package excluding one-time elements — **flag: sources are not perfectly reconciled on the exact total figure; treat $38.5M–$44.7M as the disclosed range** pending direct proxy verification).
- Note: the large majority of this package is performance-contingent (multi-year vesting, tied to financial/stock-price benchmarks), consistent with Disney's historical CEO pay structure design (see Iger comp structure below).

**Related 8-K**: Kristina Schake (SEVP, Chief Communications Officer) announced departure from the company effective after March 18, 2026, coinciding with the end of Iger's tenure — a member of Iger's inner circle stepping down alongside the CEO transition (source: WebSearch extraction of Feb 24, 2026 8-K).

## Bob Iger (Outgoing CEO) — Compensation Structure (FY2025, as disclosed in this 2026 proxy)
Source: [CEOPayWatch Disney company page](https://www.ceopaywatch.com/company/walt-disney), [CEOPayWatch Iger individual page](https://www.ceopaywatch.com/executive/bob-iger).
- **97% of Iger's target total direct annual compensation was variable/at-risk**, tied to company and stock-price performance.
- Target annual equity award composition: 60% Performance-Based Units (PBUs), 40% stock options.
- Overall target total direct comp breakdown: 49% PBUs, 32% stock options, 16% target annual incentive, plus base salary.
- PBU payout depends on 3-year achievement of: relative Total Shareholder Return (TSR), Adjusted EPS Growth, and ROIC performance.
- Options have value only if Disney's stock price increases after grant date.
- Total FY2025 pay figure cited by one aggregator: ~$45.8 million total pay, "458:1 worker pay ratio" (CEOPayWatch) — **this figure is from a third-party pay-ratio tracking site, not independently confirmed against the primary proxy table; treat as approximate pending direct verification.**
