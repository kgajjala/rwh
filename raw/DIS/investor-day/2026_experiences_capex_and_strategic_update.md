## 4. Corporate Structure Moves

### (a) Hulu — full buyout of Comcast/NBCUniversal stake (COMPLETED)
- Original 2023 framework: Disney agreed to pay Comcast **$8.61 billion** for NBCUniversal's
  remaining ~33% Hulu stake, representing 1/3 of a **$27.5 billion guaranteed floor valuation** agreed
  in 2019.
- Valuation dispute: independent appraisers disagreed — Disney's appraiser valued Hulu **below** the
  guaranteed floor; Comcast/NBCU's appraiser valued it **substantially above**. Went to a third/
  arbitration process.
- **Final arbitrated true-up payment: $438.7 million** (on top of the ~$8.61B already paid), bringing
  **total Disney payout for full Hulu ownership to just over $9 billion**.
- **Deal closed / Disney took full 100% ownership by 2025-07-24.**
- Source: [Disney to pay Comcast $438.7 million to take full control of Hulu, ending lengthy valuation process — CNBC, 2025-06-09](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/06/09/disney-comcast-hulu-valuation.html)
- Source: [Disney to Finalize Hulu Buyout by July 24 After Arbitration Sets Final Price at $438.7 Million](https://www.subscriptioninsider.com/article-type/news/disney-to-finalize-hulu-buyout-by-july-24-after-arbitration-sets-final-price-at-438-7-million)
- Source: [Disney Closes Hulu Deal With Comcast, Paying Billions Less Than NBCU Was Seeking — Variety](https://variety.com/2025/tv/news/disney-closes-hulu-deal-comcast-price-tag-1236423903/)

### (b) India — Reliance/JioStar joint venture (COMPLETED, ownership stakes confirmed)
- **Structure**: merger of Disney's Star India media business + Reliance's Viacom18/JioCinema
  business, forming a new JV (initially branded "JioStar," consumer streaming product "JioHotstar").
- **Completed / became effective: November 2024**, following NCLT Mumbai, Competition Commission of
  India, and other regulatory approvals.
- **Valuation**: JV valued at **₹70,352 crore (~US$8.5 billion)** post-money, excluding synergies.
- **Ownership split**: Reliance Industries Limited (RIL) directly **16.34%**; Viacom18 (RIL step-down
  unit) **46.82%**; **Disney 36.84%**. (I.e., Reliance/RIL group controls **63.16%** combined; Disney
  is a **minority JV partner** at 36.84%.)
- **Governance**: JV chaired by **Nita Ambani**.
- **Consumer product**: JioCinema + Disney+ Hotstar merged into a single streaming platform,
  **JioHotstar**, launched **February 2025**.
- **Late-2025 internal restructuring** (secondary, does not change Disney's 36.84% JV stake): Reliance
  completed a merger of Star Television Productions Ltd. (STPL) into JioStar, effective **2025-11-30**
  — this absorbed a Disney-era subsidiary brand entity into the JV's direct corporate structure; framed
  in coverage as "JioStar takes direct control of the 'Star' brand." This is an internal
  simplification, not a change in the RIL/Viacom18/Disney ownership percentages above.
- Source: [Reliance and Disney Announce Completion of Transaction... — JioStar official](https://www.jiostar.com/news/reliance-and-disney-announce-completion-of-transaction-to-form-joint-venture-to-bring-together-the-most-iconic-and-engaging-entertainment-brands-in-india/)
- Source: [Reliance, Disney complete merger; form Rs 70,352 crore joint venture — Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/reliance-disney-complete-merger-form-rs-70-352-crore-joint-venture-124111401609_1.html)
- Source: [Reliance completes Star Television-JioStar merger — The Week, 2025-12-02](https://www.theweek.in/news/biz-tech/2025/12/02/reliance-completes-star-television-jiostar-merger.html)
- Source: [Walt Disney Co - Form 8-K - FY2024 (deal-completion exhibit, SEC EDGAR)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001744489/000119312524049404/d800554dex991.htm)

### (c) Venu Sports JV (Fox / WBD / Disney sports-streaming bundle) — CALLED OFF / CONFIRMED DEAD
- Originally announced Feb 2024 as a joint sports-streaming bundle combining Fox, Warner Bros.
  Discovery, and Disney/ESPN live-sports rights; initial pricing target ~$42.99/month.
- Blocked by a **preliminary injunction** shortly before planned launch (timed to 2024 NFL season
  kickoff), following an antitrust suit from Fubo alleging the bundle would "substantially lessen
  competition."
- **Officially discontinued: announced 2025-01-10** — the three partners jointly stated: "After
  careful consideration, we have collectively agreed to discontinue the Venu Sports joint venture and
  not launch the streaming service." Each partner had sunk **>$400 million** into the venture.
- **Contemporaneous/related move**: Disney separately announced a deal to **merge Hulu + Live TV with
  Fubo** (Disney retaining majority ownership, Fubo's executive team running the combined entity) —
  and as part of that agreement, Fubo agreed to **drop its antitrust suit against Venu**, clearing the
  final legal obstacle that had already effectively killed Venu.
- **Current status (mid-2026): confirmed dead, will not relaunch** — no indication in this session's
  search results of any revival. Disney, Fox, and WBD are each pursuing **standalone** sports-
  streaming strategies instead (ESPN's own DTC app being Disney's vehicle — see §2).
- Source: [Disney, Fox, Warner Bros. Discovery Scrap Venu Sports Streamer — Variety](https://variety.com/2025/tv/news/venu-canceled-disney-fox-warner-bros-discovery-1236271592/)
- Source: [Venu is done. Here's how Fox, Disney and WBD plan to go it alone in sports streaming — CNBC, 2025-02-27](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/02/27/venu-fox-disney-wbd-sports-streaming-strategies.html)
- Source: [Disney, Fox and Warner Bros. Discovery call off plans to launch Venu sports streaming service — CNBC, 2025-01-10](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/01/10/disney-fox-and-warner-bros-discovery-call-off-plans-to-launch-venu-sports-streaming-service.html)

## 5. Litigation, Labor, Regulatory — Last ~12 Months (2025-07 through 2026-06)

### FCC / ABC broadcast-license dispute (MATERIAL, ONGOING, escalating)
- **Origin**: FCC opened an investigation into Disney/ABC's **DEI programs** in **March 2025**
  (alleged possible violations of the Communications Act of 1934 / anti-discrimination rules).
- **Escalation, April 2026**: FCC (under Chairman **Brendan Carr**) ordered **early renewal** of
  **8 ABC-owned-and-operated TV station licenses** (CA x3, IL, NY, TX, NC, PA) — years ahead of their
  normal 2028-2031 renewal schedule — explicitly tied to the DEI inquiry. Carr has publicly questioned
  whether Disney is "fit" to hold these licenses.
  - Source: [FCC launches review of Disney broadcast licenses years ahead of schedule — CNBC, 2026-04-28](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/28/fcc-begins-review-of-disney-broadcast-licenses-years-ahead-of-schedule.html)
  - Source: [FCC orders ABC to renew TV broadcast licenses early over DEI programs — The Desk](https://thedesk.net/2026/04/fcc-dei-disney-order-abc-broadcast-tv-licenses/)
- **May 2026**: Disney/ABC filed the license renewals **"under protest,"** with an accompanying legal
  objection (lead counsel Paul Clement, former U.S. Solicitor General) calling the FCC action
  **"unlawful, arbitrary, and unconstitutional"** — arguing First Amendment retaliation. ABC filed an
  extensive brief specifically for its Houston station.
  - Source: [Disney's ABC files early broadcast licenses renewal. FCC Chair vows to 'follow the facts' — CNBC, 2026-05-28](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/28/disney-fcc-broadcast-licenses-renewal.html)
  - Source: [ABC accuses Trump's FCC of 'unconstitutional retaliation' in station license fight — CNN, 2026-05-28](https://www.cnn.com/2026/05/28/media/abc-fcc-disney-licenses-unconstitutional)
  - Source: [Disney-owned ABC claims the FCC violated its First Amendment rights over 'The View' — NPR, 2026-05-09](https://www.npr.org/2026/05/09/nx-s1-5816334/disney-owned-abc-claims-the-fcc-violated-its-first-amendment-rights-over-the-view)
- Related, earlier-2025 context cited in coverage: **ABC paid $15 million to settle a defamation

### Disability Access Service (DAS) — shareholder proposal + litigation (MATERIAL, partially resolved)
- Disney narrowed DAS (theme-park disability accommodation) eligibility criteria starting ~2024,
  prompting an advocacy group ("DAS Defenders") and negative press.
- **Pending class-action lawsuit** exists (contends new policies are "too restrictive") — status/
