## Headline Financials

- **Total revenue**: $23.7 billion, up 2% YoY from $23.2 billion in Q3 FY2024. [Source](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/disney-q3-earnings-surpass-estimates-revenues-increase-y-y)
- **Income before income taxes**: $3.2 billion, up 4% YoY from $3.1 billion. [Source](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/walt-disney-company-reports-third-104000004.html)
- **Diluted EPS (GAAP)**: $2.92, vs. $1.43 in Q3 FY2024.
- **Adjusted EPS**: $1.61, up 16% YoY from $1.39 (beat Zacks consensus by 10.3%). [Source](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/disney-q3-earnings-surpass-estimates-revenues-increase-y-y)
- **Total segment operating income**: $4.6 billion, up 8% YoY from $4.2 billion.

## Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) / Streaming

- **DTC operating income**: increased $365 million YoY to $346 million.
- **Disney+ and Hulu combined subscriptions**: 183 million, up 2.6 million vs. Q2 FY2025.
- **Disney+ subscribers**: 128 million, up 1.8 million vs. Q2 FY2025.
- **Hulu SVOD-only ARPU**: increased from $12.36 to $12.40.
- **Disney+ ARPU (domestic)**: $8.09/month (up from $7.70 YoY, referenced in later Q4 comparison).
- **Disney+ ARPU (international)**: $8.00/month (up from $6.78 YoY).
- **IMPORTANT DISCLOSURE-CHANGE FLAG**: Management announced during/around this quarter that Disney will **stop reporting quarterly paid-subscriber counts and ARPU figures for Disney+, Hulu, and ESPN+ starting after Q3 FY2025**. This is confirmed as the **last quarter with full subscriber/ARPU disclosure** in the standard press-release format. [Source](https://www.demandsage.com/disney-users/)
- Hulu to be fully combined into the Disney+ app in the following year per exec commentary. [Source](https://www.marketingbrew.com/stories/2025/08/06/hulu-disney-standalone-app-earnings-espn-nfl)

## Parks & Experiences Detail

- Domestic Parks & Experiences operating income grew 22% to $1.7 billion, "fueled by increased guest spending and stronger attendance."
- International revenue: $1.7 billion (up from $1.5 billion in 2024, +10% YoY); international operating income: $375 million (up from $299 million, +25% YoY).
- Per-guest spending at Walt Disney World and Disneyland up 5% YoY, driven by admissions, food & beverage, merchandise growth.
- Operating income included a ~$40 million benefit from Easter holiday timing and a ~$30 million negative impact from Disney Cruise Line pre-opening expenses (new ship ramp).
- [Source](https://worldofwalt.com/disney-earnings-q3-2025.html)

## Guidance Given (for Q4 FY2025)

- Total segment operating income guided to decrease ~5% for Q4 FY2025 to ~$3.5 billion (vs. $3.7 billion in Q4 FY2024).
- Full-year streaming business guided to deliver **$1.3 billion in operating income** for FY2025.
- Experiences segment operating income guided to **grow 8%** for the full year.
- Disney+/Hulu total subscriptions guided to increase **more than 10 million** vs. Q3 FY2025 by Q4, majority from Hulu (expanded Charter distribution deal); modest increase expected in Disney+ subscribers specifically.
- [Source](https://news.alphastreet.com/disney-dis-heads-into-q2-fy2026-with-streaming-profit-in-focus/amp/) (retrospective confirmation) and contemporaneous guidance summaries.

## ESPN / Sports Notes

- This is the **last quarter before ESPN's standalone DTC app launch** (which occurred August 21, 2025, just after this earnings date but within the following fiscal quarter, Q4 FY2025).
- NFL deal (10% equity stake to NFL in exchange for NFL Network/RedZone rights) and WWE deal (exclusive US rights to WrestleMania, Royal Rumble, SummerSlam from 2026) were both under discussion/announced around this period as strategic underpinning for ESPN's DTC launch. [Source](https://9to5mac.com/2025/08/06/espn-standalone-subscription-service-launch-date-price/)
