## Headline Financials — Q4 FY2025

- **Q4 revenue**: $22.5 billion, comparable to (flat vs.) Q4 FY2024.
- **Income before income taxes**: $2.0 billion, up from $0.9 billion in Q4 FY2024.
- **Diluted EPS (GAAP)**: $0.73, up from $0.25 in Q4 FY2024.
- **Adjusted EPS**: $1.11, down 3% from $1.14 in Q4 FY2024.
- **Total segment operating income**: $3.5 billion, down 5% from $3.7 billion in Q4 FY2024.

## Headline Financials — Full Year FY2025

- **FY2025 total revenue**: $94.4 billion, up 3% from $91.4 billion in FY2024.
- **Income before income taxes (FY)**: $12.0 billion, up from $7.6 billion in FY2024.
- **Diluted EPS (GAAP, FY)**: $6.85, up from $2.72 in FY2024.
- **Adjusted EPS (FY)**: $5.93, up 19% from $4.97 in FY2024.
- **Total segment operating income (FY)**: $17.6 billion, up 12% from $15.6 billion in FY2024.

## Linear Networks / Cord-Cutting Detail (Q4 FY2025)

- Linear TV segment revenue **-16%** YoY; operating income **-21%** YoY to $391 million.
- Domestic Linear Networks operating income decline drivers: lower advertising (viewership decline), political advertising headwind (~-$40 million YoY, no election-year comp), and a **-$107 million** YoY comp driven by the **Star India transaction** (Star India contributed $84 million to Q4 FY2024 results but is no longer consolidated).
- [Source](https://thedesk.net/2025/11/disney-fiscal-q4-2025-earnings-report/) and [Source](https://www.metatradingclub.com/walt-disney-q4-2025-earnings/)

## Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) / Streaming — Last Quarter With Subscriber Disclosure

- **DTC segment operating income**: +$99 million YoY to **$352 million**.
- **Disney+ and Hulu combined subscriptions**: 196 million, up 12.4 million vs. Q3 FY2025.
- **Disney+ subscribers**: 132 million, up 3.8 million vs. Q3 FY2025.
- **Hulu subscribers**: 83.7 million.
- DTC revenue +8% YoY, net of a 2-point adverse impact from Disney+ Hotstar being in the prior-year quarter's results (Hotstar since divested/restructured).
- **Disney+ ARPU (global blended)**: rose from $7.20 (Q4 FY2024) to $8.04 (Q4 FY2025), +11.7% YoY, driven by price increases and improved ad-tier revenue per subscriber.
- **CONFIRMED: this is the LAST quarter Disney reported Disney+/Hulu/ESPN+ subscriber counts and ARPU** in the standard disclosure format — subsequent quarters (Q1 FY2026, Q2 FY2026) do not report subscriber counts. [Source](https://www.thewrap.com/disney-earnings-q4-2025/) and [Source](https://www.adexchanger.com/tv/bob-igers-maybe-parting-gift-to-disney-no-more-reporting-subscriber-numbers/)

## ESPN DTC App — First Quarter of Commentary (launched mid-quarter, Aug 21, 2025)

- ESPN's standalone DTC streaming app/service ("ESPN Unlimited," $29.99/month standalone or $35.99/month bundled with Disney+/Hulu) launched **August 21, 2025** — within this Q4 FY2025 reporting quarter.
- Management (Iger) did **not disclose subscriber numbers** but said they were "pleased with what they had seen so far."
- Iger: the rate of pay-TV subscribers with access via the app using it "has been very, very encouraging."
- Company said it was attracting more advertising and new advertisers to the ESPN app.
- Short-form, user-generated content features "have already worked" on the new ESPN app per Iger.
- Iger characterized the ESPN app as "a very positive step for the future of ESPN," calling it important for "solidifying the business going forward."
- [Source](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/business/business-news/disney-earnings-streaming-subscribers-grow-1236425508/) and [Source](https://www.adexchanger.com/tv/bob-igers-maybe-parting-gift-to-disney-no-more-reporting-subscriber-numbers/)

## Capital Allocation / FY2026 Guidance Given at This Call

- Board declared cash dividend of **$1.50/share** (payable January and July 2026), up from $1.00/share in FY2025.
- **Share repurchase target doubled to $7 billion for FY2026** (vs. FY2025 level).
- FY2026 guidance: **double-digit adjusted EPS growth** reiterated for FY2026, and again for FY2027.
- Note: FY2026 (Disney's fiscal year) includes a **53rd week** — guidance explicitly does NOT include the benefit of this extra week unless stated otherwise.
- [Source](https://variety.com/2025/biz/news/disney-q4-2025-earnings-revenue-flat-earnings-outlook-streaming-1236580686/)
