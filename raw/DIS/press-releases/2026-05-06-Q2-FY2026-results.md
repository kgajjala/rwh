## Headline Financials

- **Total revenue**: $25.2 billion, up 7% YoY from $23.6 billion in Q2 FY2025 (beat consensus of ~$24.85B).
- **Income before income taxes**: $3.4 billion, up 9% YoY from $3.1 billion.
- **Total segment operating income**: $4.6 billion, up 4% YoY.
- **Diluted EPS (GAAP)**: $1.27, down from $1.81 in Q2 FY2025.
- **Adjusted EPS**: $1.57, up from $1.45 in Q2 FY2025 (beat consensus of $1.50).
- Stock jumped ~7-8% on the report.
- [Source](https://variety.com/2026/tv/news/disney-q2-2026-earnings-josh-damaro-streaming-income-1236738974/) and [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/06/disney-dis-earnings-q2-2026.html)

## Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) / Streaming — Second Quarter Without Subscriber Disclosure

- Still no Disney+/Hulu/ESPN+ subscriber counts disclosed (consistent with policy change effective after Q3 FY2025).
- **Disney+ and Hulu combined revenue**: $5.49 billion, **+13% YoY** — acceleration from Q1's +11%.
- **Disney+/Hulu operating income**: **$582 million, +88% YoY** — driven by price hikes implemented fall 2025.
- Streaming continues to deliver **double-digit SVOD operating margins**, consistent with the 10% FY2026 target reaffirmed at Q1.
- D'Amaro touted Disney+ as the "digital centerpiece" of the company on this call.
- [Source](https://variety.com/2026/tv/news/disney-q2-2026-earnings-josh-damaro-streaming-income-1236738974/) and [Source](https://www.thewrap.com/industry-news/business/disney-earnings-q2-2026/)

## ESPN DTC App

- ESPN's DTC app continues to be "a bright spot" — digital subscriber revenue "more than offsetting declines in the traditional TV ecosystem" per commentary.
- D'Amaro highlighted app enhancements: **Multiview, Verts, SportsCenter for You** — cited as making the offering "increasingly compelling for fans."
- Framing from management: ESPN/Sports is in an "earlier monetization transition," having "just launched ESPN Unlimited last year" — implies Sports segment profitability lags Entertainment's DTC transition by roughly a year.
- [Source](https://variety.com/2026/tv/news/disney-q2-2026-earnings-josh-damaro-streaming-income-1236738974/) and [Source](https://news.alphastreet.com/the-walt-disney-company-dis-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)

## Linear Networks / Cord-Cutting

- Linear revenues continue to decline (industry-wide cord-cutting), but management frames Disney Entertainment as a whole as still growing nicely — **guided to double-digit segment operating income growth this fiscal year** (excluding the 53rd week impact), calling Disney Entertainment "one of the faster-growing media businesses" despite the linear drag.
- Outside commentary (analyst framing, not direct Disney quote): "linear-networks drag remains real... any sharper weakness in affiliate fees or advertising would remind investors that this transition is still incomplete."
- [Source](https://tvnewscheck.com/business/article/disney-sees-streaming-as-a-hedge-against-declining-linear/)

## Parks & Experiences Detail

- Per-capita guest spending at domestic parks **+5% YoY**.
- Domestic parks attendance **-1% YoY**, attributed in part to continued softness in international visitation comparisons.
- Management guidance: expects domestic park attendance **to improve YoY in Q3** vs. Q2 results (i.e., comps easing).
- Cruise Line: capacity expansion continuing — Disney Adventure (first Asia-based ship) launched; long-term target of **13 ships by 2031**, doubling to **8 ships / 10,100 rooms around 2026**.
- FY2026 CapEx composition: majority earmarked toward capacity expansion — new cruise ship, major expansions at Walt Disney World (Orlando), Disneyland (Anaheim), and Shanghai Disney Resort. Capital-light international expansion also flagged: new cruise ship JV with Oriental Land Company (Japan), new theme park with Miral (Abu Dhabi).
- Management characterized current domestic demand as "healthy," while "mindful of macroeconomic uncertainty consumers are facing."

## Guidance Given (FY2026 Full Year, reaffirmed/raised at this call)

- FY2026 adjusted EPS growth guided to **~12% excluding the 53rd week**, and **~16% including the 53rd week** impact.
- **Share buyback target raised from $7 billion to at least $8 billion** for FY2026 (increase from the Q4 FY2025/Q1 FY2026 guided level).
- Management reaffirmed **double-digit adjusted EPS growth into FY2027** as well.

- This was **Josh D'Amaro's first earnings call as CEO** (effective since March 18, 2026); Bob Iger continues as senior adviser/board member through his planned retirement on **December 31, 2026**.
- D'Amaro used the call to introduce himself to Wall Street and lay out strategic priorities — described by Hollywood Reporter as "Josh D'Amaro Lays Out Disney Strategy in First Earnings Call as CEO."
- Disney CFO (Hugh Johnston) separately addressed the smoothness of the succession on/around this period.
- The stock's ~7-8% pop on the print was characterized partly as a positive market reaction to D'Amaro's first outing.
