## Verbatim / near-verbatim quotes recovered

**CEO Bob Iger, on FY2024**:
> "Amid ongoing and unprecedented industry disruptions, we have emerged from a period of considerable
> challenges well positioned for growth and optimistic about our future."

Letter characterized FY2024 as "pivotal and successful." Singled out Disney Entertainment's "historic
year at the box office" driven by *Inside Out 2*, *Deadpool & Wolverine*, and *Moana 2*. Emphasized:
"creative excellence remains at the center of our work as we build our business and seek to drive
shareholder value," with commitment to "continue to strive to keep high quality and bold storytelling
front and center."

**Chairman James Gorman, on FY2024 (from the accompanying letter)**:
> "The Walt Disney Company delivered strong financial results for fiscal 2024, achieving notable
> strategic and creative milestones in each of the Company's segments. This once again demonstrates
> the continued progress Disney's world-class leadership team is making in executing on the Company's
> strategic plan under Bob Iger's exceptional leadership as Chief Executive Officer."
> "The Board is grateful for your support throughout the year. As we look forward, we have strong
> confidence in the management team's plans for continued success."

Gorman's letter also reiterated the board's commitment to naming Iger's successor "in early 2026"
(succession context — Gorman became Board Chairman effective 2025-01-02, succeeding Mark Parker in
that role; see [Board Names James P. Gorman as Chairman press release](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/james-gorman-disney-chairman-board/)).
