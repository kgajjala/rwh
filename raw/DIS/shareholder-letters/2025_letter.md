FY2025 Annual Financial Report and Q4 FY25 earnings materials. This is Disney's shareholder-letter

## Verbatim / near-verbatim quotes recovered (via secondary sourcing)

**CEO Bob Iger, on FY2025 results** (as quoted in secondary reporting of the annual report):
> "This was another year of great progress as we strengthened the company by leveraging the value of
> our creative and brand assets and continued to make meaningful progress in our direct-to-consumer
> businesses."
> "Our strategy, coupled with our portfolio of complementary businesses and a strong balance sheet,
> enables us to continue investing in high-quality offerings for our consumers and increasing our
> returns to shareholders."

**Chairman James Gorman letter (accompanying FY2025 proxy)** — reflects on FY2025 accomplishments;
per secondary reporting: "through Disney's strategic vision and collection of businesses, they
continue to tell stories reaching more people in more ways than ever before," with gratitude to
leadership team and shareholders. Reiterated board's commitment to naming Iger's CEO successor "in
early 2026" (this was subsequently delivered — see 2026 succession note below; D'Amaro named
2026-02-03).

## Fiscal 2025 context cited in Q4 FY25 earnings call (2025-11-13), management commentary
- Adjusted EPS for FY2025 up 19% YoY; ~19% adjusted-EPS CAGR over the prior three fiscal years.
- Streaming/DTC: "another quarter of profit growth" — explicitly contrasted against the ~$4B DTC
  operating loss three years prior (i.e., FY2022), framed as validation of the turnaround strategy.
- Revenue FY2025: $94.4B (+3% / +$3.1B YoY). Net income attributable to Disney: $12.4B (+$7.4B YoY).
