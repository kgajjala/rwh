## ESPN DTC App — First Detailed Management Commentary

This is the first earnings call after the Aug 21, 2025 ESPN app launch, so it carries the most substantive early-performance commentary found across all four quarters:

- Iger, on the app not disclosing subscriber numbers: management was **"pleased with what they had seen so far."**
- Iger, on pay-TV subscribers using the app: the rate of engagement **"has been very, very encouraging."**
- Company noted it was attracting **more advertising and new advertisers** to the ESPN app specifically (beyond legacy linear ad relationships).
- Iger said short-form, user-generated content **"has already worked"** on the new ESPN app — used as a proof point for bringing similar features to Disney+ (this theme recurs and is expanded upon in the Q1 FY2026 call re: the OpenAI/Sora partnership).
- Iger characterized the ESPN app overall as **"a very positive step for the future of ESPN,"** important for "solidifying the business going forward."

## Disclosure Policy Change — Confirmed on/around This Call

- Disney confirmed it will **stop reporting Disney+, Hulu, and ESPN+ subscriber numbers** going forward — described in press coverage as "Bob Iger's (maybe) parting gift to Disney" (i.e., framed as a legacy-defining disclosure change made under his tenure, ahead of the succession).
- This is consistent with what was flagged as coming "after Q3 FY2025" — Q4 FY2025 results (this call) were the last to include the full subscriber breakdown; going forward, story shifts to profitability metrics only.
