## Succession Context (Important — Timing)

- This call took place **one day before** Disney publicly announced Josh D'Amaro as Iger's successor (announced Feb 3, 2026; effective March 18, 2026). Succession was NOT explicitly discussed as a done-deal on this call, but analysts probed forward-looking, succession-adjacent questions.
- Analyst question (paraphrased per search synthesis): asked whether Iger sees any areas his successor could "jump start" that would drive the business for the long term.
- **Iger's response**: the company is **"in better shape now than three years ago."**
- Iger, on Parks vs. Entertainment internal dynamic: described "healthy competition" between the two divisions over **"which is going to essentially prevail as the number one driver of profitability for the company."**
- Iger, on the Parks business specifically: **"As I look ahead, I actually am very, very bullish on that business and its ability to grow."** Also: **"When you look at the footprint of the business today, it's never been more broad or more diverse, and the projects that we have underway are going to make it even more so."**
