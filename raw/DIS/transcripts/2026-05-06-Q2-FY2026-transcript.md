## ESPN DTC Strategy Commentary

- D'Amaro highlighted specific app feature enhancements as drivers of engagement: **Multiview, Verts, SportsCenter for You** — framed as making ESPN's offering "increasingly compelling for fans."
- Management framing on Sports segment profitability lag: Sports/ESPN is in an **"earlier monetization transition"**, having "just launched ESPN Unlimited last year" (i.e., roughly a year behind Entertainment's DTC profitability curve) — this is a useful direct explanation for why Sports op income is still declining YoY while Entertainment SVOD is up 88%.
- Guidance: ESPN operating income expected to **decline ~14% YoY in Q3 FY2026** (June quarter) due to double-digit programming expense growth, including new rights-agreement timing.
- [Source](https://variety.com/2026/tv/news/disney-q2-2026-earnings-josh-damaro-streaming-income-1236738974/) and [Source](https://news.alphastreet.com/disney-dis-heads-into-q2-fy2026-with-streaming-profit-in-focus/amp/)

## CapEx Discussion

- Management confirmed FY2026 Experiences CapEx is dominated by: the new cruise ship ramp, and major expansions at Walt Disney World (Orlando), Disneyland (Anaheim), and Shanghai Disney Resort.
- Longer-term framing: "the majority of CapEx is earmarked for investments that are expanding capacity" over the next decade — a direct, if brief, articulation of the capex-ROI logic (capacity expansion as the primary use of funds, not maintenance).
- [Source](https://news.alphastreet.com/disney-dis-heads-into-q2-fy2026-with-streaming-profit-in-focus/amp/)

## Linear Networks Q&A Framing

- Management position: linear revenue continues to decline, but Disney Entertainment as a whole is still guided to **double-digit segment operating income growth** for FY2026 (ex-53rd week) — used to counter the narrative that linear decline is dragging down the whole Entertainment segment.
