## OFF-SCRIPT DISCLOSURES (not in the press release)

- Fast Break: surpassed the ~250-store back-to-school goal; guided to "300 to 350 Fast Break stores
  globally by year-end."
- ScoreCard+: new paid loyalty tier — "a paid tier designed for our most engaged athletes who will
  pay $99 per year for membership." (Hobart)
- Q3 gross margin pressure characterized as "most pronounced."
- Promotional environment expected to "remain challenging at least through the fourth quarter." (Stack)
- NO buyback commentary despite $3.0B remaining authorization and a 30.7% single-day decline.
- NO timeline given for Foot Locker's return to profitability.

## ED STACK (Executive Chairman)

On the promotional cycle:
  "The inventory built up across the industry supply chains and across the retail marketplace, which
   led to an increasingly aggressive promotional environment."
  "We expect the broader promotional environment, particularly around legacy silhouettes, to remain
   challenging at least through the fourth quarter."

On EMEA / Foot Locker International:
  "The industry is carrying too much inventory and the consumer has been even more cautious than
   expected due to the geopolitical environment."

On demand rotation:
  "The consumer is looking for products that are new, innovative, different... some of these older
   legacy silhouettes and franchises that have done so well have slowed."
  "The footwear business is not going away... the footwear business is going to continue to be
   extremely important to this industry."

On Fast Break performance:
  "Fast Break stores have definitely outperformed the traditional legacy stores... north of 300, 350
   stores by the end of the year globally."

On investing counter-cyclically:
  "I don't think there's a better time to do this right now when the market is in a bit of turmoil...
   when things come out the other side, we'll have that market share."

On the turnaround stage:
  "We're still early in the Foot Locker turnaround... we believe the long-term opportunity at Foot
   Locker remains strong."

## LAUREN HOBART (President & CEO)

  "Our growth outpaced the broader industry by nearly 200 basis points, reinforcing our ability to
   strengthen our leadership position and gain market share."
  "During Q2, we opened 5 House of Sport locations and 8 Field House locations... we expect to open
   approximately 14 total House of Sport locations and 20 total Field House locations."
  "We introduced ScoreCard+, a paid tier designed for our most engaged athletes who will pay $99 per
   year for membership."
  "Our ScoreCard loyalty program... connects us with approximately 30 million active athletes."
  "While we are taking a more cautious view of the balance of the year, we remain highly confident in
   the strength of the DICK'S Business and our long-term opportunity at Foot Locker."

## NAVDEEP GUPTA (CFO)

Guidance bridge:
  "Consolidated non-GAAP earnings per diluted share in the range of $11 to $12 compared to our prior
   range of $13.50 to $14.50."
  "Operating loss for the Foot Locker business in the range of $80 million to $40 million compared to
   prior expectations of $110 million to $150 million in profit."
  Full-year Foot Locker proforma comps: "negative 2% to flat compared to... 1.5% to 3% growth."

DICK'S margin:
  "DICK'S business, gross margin expanded 79 basis points versus last year" — driven by DICK'S Media
  Network, GameChanger, and tariff refunds offsetting promotional pressure.
  FY operating margin guide cut to 10.6%-10.9% from 11.0%-11.4%, attributed to a "more promotional
  marketplace" plus "higher expected fuel prices and supply chain expenses."

Synergies / tariffs:
  "We remain confident in achieving our previously announced $100 million to $125 million of cost
   synergies over the medium term."
  "We received approximately $59 million of tariff refunds... a benefit of approximately $21 million
   was included in our non-GAAP results for Q2."

Capex: ~$1.4B net for 2026, split roughly 70/30 between DICK'S and Foot Locker.
