# Sources (cross-checked per R9):

# Context: first unscripted appearance since the Aug 25 crash (-30.7%). Was the standing "next trigger."

## FOOT LOCKER
Diagnosis framed as assortment, not demand:
  Hobart: "overcapacity in older styles and undercapacity in new products."
  Legacy Air Force 1s and Dunks have slowed; innovative variants remain hard to keep in stock.
  Hobart: Foot Locker "needs little additional capital" and is not draining resources from core DICK'S.
  Acquisition described as "creating a menu that all of you would expect us to create" rather than forcing outcomes.
On Europe — blunter than the Q2 release:
  Hobart: "EMEA has been very difficult. When we originally gave our guidance... there was not a war going
  on in the Middle East. That has had a really meaningful impact on Europe."
NOT SAID: any Foot Locker profitability date. Still absent, as at the Q2 call.

## DICK'S CORE
Comparable-store-sales guidance for the DICK'S banner kept UNCHANGED despite Q2 promotional pressure.
  Hobart: "Our business on the DICK'S side is really very good."
  Hobart, pushing back on the category bears: the idea that "the athletic cycle is over is overdone."
  Stack: the DICK'S consumer "is holding up very well" across income cohorts, with "no trade-down" observed,
  only growth across demographics.

## CAPITAL ALLOCATION
No commentary located on the $3.0B share repurchase authorization, on free cash flow, or on debt.
NOTE: eight days later (Sep 22) the company launched a two-tranche senior notes offering whose stated
uses include repurchases of common stock. The absence of any capital-structure discussion at this
fireside is recorded as a gap, not an inference.
