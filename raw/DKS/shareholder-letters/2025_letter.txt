Dear Fellow Shareholders:
This past year was a defining chapter for DICK’S, Inc., marked by growth and significant transformation across our 
business. 
Within the DICK’S Business, we delivered strong financial results, expanded our market share, scaled our innovative 
store concepts and digital platforms, and continued to elevate our best-in-class athlete and teammate experiences. 
We also took a bold and strategic step forward with the acquisition of Foot Locker, transforming DICK’S into a new 
global sports retail platform ready to serve new athletes and consumers across the world with an industry-leading 
omni-channel experience.
While DICK’S and Foot Locker each have distinct brands, strategies, and near-term priorities, we are united by a 
shared focus on the fundamentals: compelling and differentiated product assortments, powerful athlete and consumer 
experiences, deep brand engagement, and an unwavering commitment to our teammates. Our confidence in these 
strategies – and in the long-term value they create – continues to grow.
The DICK’S Business
Performance in the DICK’S Business in 2025 was driven by an active, engaged, and loyal athlete base. Athletes 
continue to prioritize healthy, active lifestyles and increasingly turn to DICK’S to meet their needs. They care deeply 
about performance, innovation, and authenticity and they have been willing to invest in differentiated product that 
delivers on those expectations. 
In 2025, the DICK’S Business: 
• Delivered record sales of $14.11 billion, an increase of 5% compared to 2024.
• Achieved full-year comparable sales growth of 4.5% and non-GAAP earnings per diluted share of $14.58, up 
3.8% year over year. 
• Delivered over 40 basis points of gross margin expansion compared to 2024.
• Gained nearly 50 basis points of market share, driven by strong performance across our priority categories.
• Served approximately 30 million active Scorecard members who accounted for more than 75% of sales.
Our expanding ecosystem enables us to support athletes wherever they engage with us, building deeper connections, 
lasting loyalty and incremental growth. This year we: 
• Expanded our innovative store concepts – House of Sport and DICK’S Field House – as we continue to 
redefine sports retail. These stores connect deeply with athletes through immersive experiences while 
providing an unmatched platform for our strategic brand partners to bring innovation to life and for emerging 
brands to test, learn and grow. These stores continue to generate unprecedented interest from landlords, 
giving us access to some of the best retail locations in the country. 
• Saw tremendous growth in our e-Commerce business and app, which serves our most loyal athletes and 
drives engagement through launches, exclusive access, and experiences like MOVE.
• Continued to expand GameChanger’s position as a market leader in the multibillion-dollar sports tech space. 
Generating nearly $150 million in sales in 2025 and close to a 40% sales CAGR since 2017, GameChanger 
continues to drive strong growth, while connecting families and communities through sport when they tune 
into the most important game of the week – their loved one’s youth baseball, softball, basketball or soccer 
game. 
•
enabling our brand partners to connect with our highly engaged athletes. 
These results demonstrate our disciplined focus on serving athletes and reinforcing DICK’S as a destination for 
athletes at every stage of their journey. 
Continued scaling our DICK’S Media Network, which generated nearly 20 billion impressions in 2025, 
Our team is united around our common purpose: ensuring athletes feel confident and excited before, during, and after 
they engage with our team, products, and experiences. We're investing time and resources to show up consistently 
across channels, anticipate athletes’ needs and deliver the right solutions. 
We’re also rooted in sport at a time when the connection between sport and culture is at an all-time high. We are 
amidst one of the most dynamic periods for sport, athletes are increasingly looking for in the products and 
experiences that fuel their passions and connect them to the game – and DICK’S is uniquely positioned to meet them 
there. 
Looking ahead to 2026, we expect to deliver continued comparable sales growth, strategic expansion of our square 
footage, and strong profitability for the DICK’S Business. While we remain mindful of macroeconomic and geopolitical 
uncertainty, we are confident in our strategies, the strength of our offering, and the powerful momentum of our 
business. 
The Foot Locker Business
We completed the acquisition of Foot Locker in September 2025, and our excitement – and conviction – in the 
long-term opportunity at Foot Locker continues to grow. The combination of our two companies gives us more scale, 
deeper relationships with the most important brands in our industry, access to consumers we didn't reach before, and 
a global footprint. 
For Foot Locker, the benefits of our combination come through in improved relationships with key brands, stronger 
execution of retail fundamentals and deeper investments to better serve consumers and support the Foot Locker 
teammates. 
Since September, we have assembled a world-class management team, seen strong alignment and renewed 
engagement from our brand partners, and as part of our "clean out the garage" efforts, completed a successful 
inventory clean-up that positions the business well for fiscal 2026.
We also launched an 11-store pilot, called our “Fast Break” initiative, with clearer storytelling, better presentation, and 
a more focused assortment. Early results have been very encouraging, and we plan to expand the concept to 
approximately 250 stores by the back-to-school season.
Back to school is also when we expect an inflection point in both sales and profitability for the Foot Locker Business. 
In addition, we remain confident in achieving our previously announced $100 million to $125 million in cost synergies 
over the medium term.
At DICK'S, we've built an industry-leading business by focusing on product, performance, innovation and customer 
loyalty, always with a long-term view. We're applying that same proven playbook to the Foot Locker Business and 
making the choices we believe will create the most long-term value for our shareholders. We are confident that Foot 
Locker will reemerge as a stronger, more resilient, and more dynamic business. We will do this with the same grit, 
vision, and focus on execution that got DICK'S to where it is today.
Investing for Growth and Returning Capital
As we look to the future, we are investing where we see the greatest opportunity to gain market share and create 
long-term value. For DICK’S, this includes continuing to provide an on-trend, differentiated product assortment, 
additional store growth, relocations and remodels, and investments in technology and our supply chain. For Foot 
Locker, our focus remains on optimizing the assortment, reenergizing stores, introducing compelling marketing and 
investing in teammates.
At the same time, our strong financial position allows us to return significant capital to shareholders. Over the past 
three years, we have returned approximately $2.4 billion to shareholders. For 2026, we announced a 3% increase in 
our quarterly dividend to an expected annualized payout of $5 per share, marking the 12th consecutive year of 
dividend growth.
            
We are extremely optimistic about the future. With clear goals, proven strategies, and the confidence that comes from 
demonstrated success, we’re accelerating our efforts to deliver athlete- and consumer-led experiences that sustain 
our momentum for DICK'S and return Foot Locker to its rightful place in the industry. 
I want to thank our 100,000 teammates across all of our banners who continue to redefine retail. None of our success 
is possible without your passion, commitment and relentless focus on execution.
And to our shareholders, thank you for your continued trust and interest in DICK’S, Inc.
Sincerely,
Lauren R. Hobart
President and Chief Executive Officer
DICK’S, Inc.
