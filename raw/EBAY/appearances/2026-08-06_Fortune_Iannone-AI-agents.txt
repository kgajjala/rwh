JAMIE IANNONE (CEO, eBay) — FORTUNE INTERVIEW
Published 2026-08-06, the day after Q2 results.
Source: https://fortune.com/2026/08/06/ebay-ceo-jamie-iannone-ai-tools-ai-buyers-shopping-agents-earnings/
Captured: 2026-09-07 (extracted text)

HEADLINE DISCLOSURE — not in the filings:
eBay has BANNED autonomous third-party AI shopping agents from its marketplace,
while pushing sellers toward eBay's own AI tooling. The stated concern is agents
that "autonomously scrape sites and buy on behalf of customers" and that "could
disrupt retail marketplaces like eBay."

This is a direct strategic answer to the agentic-commerce risk eBay itself first
named as a risk factor in the FY2025 10-K. It is a CLOSED-platform stance: eBay is
declining to be an inventory API for third-party agents.

QUOTES (Iannone, to Fortune)
On eBay's own "magical" listing tool:
  "You just basically hold your phone up to something and AI guides you"
  (on describing, pricing and shipping an item).
  "When a user uses magical listings, we see 50% more listings per lister in the
   U.S. and the value of those customers using it is greater."
On authentication:
  "We've authenticated over 15 million items... to the best of my knowledge,
   we've never had a counterfeit get through."

Also referenced: Depop closed at $1.4B; full-year guidance raised after Q2
(revenue +15% to $3.1B; net income +51% to $552M).
