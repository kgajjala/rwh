TIMELINE
2026-05-01  Press reports GameStop is preparing a bid. EBAY rises sharply.
2026-05-03/04  Ryan Cohen unveils a non-binding proposal to acquire all eBay common
            stock GameStop does not already own at $125.00 per share, roughly half
            cash and half GameStop stock. Implied value ~$56B. GameStop shares fall;
            Cohen gives a combative CNBC interview.
2026-05-12  eBay's board REJECTS the proposal, calling it "neither credible nor
            attractive." Stated objections: the financing structure, governance of
            the combined company, and Cohen's own economic incentives.
2026-06-17  eBay annual meeting. A shareholder proposal to lower the ownership
            threshold required to call a special meeting FAILS: 156.9M for vs
            210.1M against (~42.7% support). This is the mechanism an outside
            holder would use to force a vote.
2026-06-25  Reports that GameStop's CEO forwent pay, with commentary that this bears
            on the eBay effort.
2026-07-16  Cohen states GameStop will pursue eBay "one way or another."
2026-07-17  13D/A: GameStop reports 43,390,383 shares = 9.8% of the 444M shares
            outstanding as of 2026-04-24. Acquired via open-market purchases and
            physically settling Put/Call Pairs. The filing states "no portion of the
            purchase price ... was borrowed by the Reporting Person for the purpose
            of acquiring, holding, trading or voting any securities."
2026-07-19/20  FT-sourced material: Cohen "keeping all our options on the table,"
            and suggests moving to a shareholder vote if the board stays unengaged.
            Cohen's own "half cash, half stock" description later characterized on
            Wall Street as "a meme."
2026-08-10  Bloomberg: Cohen is WEIGHING WITHDRAWING the $56B bid entirely and
            replacing it with a partnership or joint venture instead — eBay would
            leverage GameStop's ~1,600 US retail locations to build physical
            presence in trading cards and collectibles. GameStop would seek seats
            on eBay's board as part of any such arrangement. No final decision.
            Cohen kept buying through the summer; stake ~9.75-9.8%, making GameStop
            eBay's second-largest holder behind Vanguard's index funds.

STATUS AS OF 2026-09-07
No definitive agreement. No formal withdrawal. Bid neither accepted nor dead.
EBAY closed 2026-09-04 at $103.41 — 17.3% BELOW the $125 proposal.
