PROPOSAL 1 — Election of 11 directors. All elected.
  Notable: Adriane M. Brown drew 24.2M votes AGAINST; every other director
  received under 1.1M against.

PROPOSAL 2 — Ratification of PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP as auditor for FY2026.
  PASSED. 351.6M for / 49.9M against / 804,521 abstain.

PROPOSAL 3 — Advisory say-on-pay. APPROVED.
  323.5M for / 43.6M against / 958,769 abstain.

PROPOSAL 4 — Stockholder proposal to lower the ownership threshold required for
  stockholders to call a special meeting. FAILED.
  156.9M for / 210.1M against / 1.1M abstain  => ~42.7% support.

  SIGNIFICANCE: this is the procedural mechanism an outside holder (e.g. GameStop,
  a 9.8% holder) would use to force a shareholder vote outside the annual cycle.
  It failed, but 42.7% support is high, and the vote was held five weeks after the
  board rejected the GameStop proposal.
