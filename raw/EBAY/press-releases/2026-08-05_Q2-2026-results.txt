EBAY INC. — Q2 2026 RESULTS (quarter ended 2026-06-30)

RESULTS
- Net revenue $3.1B, +15% as reported, +14% FX-neutral
- GMV $22.4B, +15% as reported, +14% FX-neutral
- GAAP EPS (continuing ops) $1.21 ; Non-GAAP EPS $1.60
- GAAP operating margin 21.6% ; Non-GAAP operating margin 28.5%
- GAAP net income $552M, +51%
- Active buyers 136M, +2% (TTM). US active buyers +6%.
  Enthusiast buyers 16M, +3%, average annual spend ~$3,600
- Advertising revenue $596M = 2.7% of GMV; first-party ads +24% organic
- Free cash flow $326M
- Share repurchases $310M (~3M shares); dividends paid $138M

STRATEGIC
- FOCUS CATEGORIES +26%, exceeding 40% OF TOTAL GMV FOR THE FIRST TIME
  (prior disclosure level was ~30%)
- C2C GMV +20%+, outpacing overall GMV
- Focus categories, C2C and recommerce EACH grew >20% and together are
  >70% of total GMV
- eBay Live GMV up ~8x YoY across seven markets. eBay did NOT disclose the
  absolute number, so scale cannot be assessed externally.
- Depop acquisition CLOSED 2026-07-30 for $1.4B cash (inclusive of adjustments),
  above the $1.2B originally announced. Expected to add ~2.5pp to Q3 GMV growth.
  CFO Peggy Alford: Depop becomes "accretive to ... operating income in 2028."

GUIDANCE
Q3 2026:
- Revenue $3.07-3.12B (+8-10% FX-neutral)
- GMV $22.0-22.4B (+10-12% FX-neutral)
- GAAP EPS $0.94-0.99 ; Non-GAAP EPS $1.36-1.42
Full year 2026 (RAISED):
- FX-neutral GMV growth 11.5-12.5%
- Consolidated revenue growth 11-12% (Depop ~1pp of it)
- Non-GAAP operating income growth 10-12% (Depop ~2pp headwind)
- Non-GAAP EPS growth 10-12%, despite ~2.5pp Depop dilution

NOTE: the release contains NO mention of the GameStop proposal.
