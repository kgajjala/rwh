EQUIFAX Q3 2026 CALL (Oct 20, 2026), Mark Begor (CEO) and John Gamble (CFO) per Motley Fool transcript
https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/20/equifax-efx-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/
- "In September, the FHFA accelerated the use of VantageScore for conforming mortgages to all U.S. mortgage lenders." About 2,000 mortgage lenders use VantageScore in underwriting, 7x since April; Equifax mortgage VantageScore volume up over 230% since April.
- "Through September, the bulk of mortgage lenders continue to purchase both the FICO mortgage score and obtain a free Vantage score from Equifax"; over 200 lenders use VantageScore exclusively; more paid conversions expected in Q4 and 2027.
- Equifax will keep VantageScore at $1 through 2028 (Begor).
- About 45% of Equifax's 2026 USIS mortgage revenue is FICO royalty pass-through at no margin.
- FHFA discussions about eliminating tri-merge for a two-bureau report; Equifax and "the vast majority of industry participants" oppose.
- Guidance cut $80M of US mortgage revenue vs July; US mortgage transaction volumes expected to fall by a high single digit percentage in 2027 vs 2026 on current run rates; $20M Q3 shortfall attributed to a 75 bp rise in 30-year mortgage rates since July.
- Guidance assumes Equifax sells FICO scores on all mortgage transactions with limited VantageScore revenue in Q4 and FY2026.

HOUSINGWIRE, "FICO holds mortgage price as analysts flag volume play" (Oct 26-27, 2026): https://www.housingwire.com/articles/fico-preferred-program-analyst-reaction/
- Preferred Program: $10 per score through tri-merge resellers for lenders and brokers who pull FICO on every relevant application; covers Classic FICO and FICO Score 10T; "flat year-over-year pricing" per FICO.
- Mizuho: program partly aimed at "waterfalling" (cheaper score first); had assumed ~5% FICO mortgage price growth in 2027, now flat. UBS: $10 excludes funding, closed-loan and success fees; takes effect on enrollment; target $942 from $1,100 (Oct 26).
- FHA will accept Classic FICO, FICO 10T and VantageScore 4.0 from Jan 1, 2027. Fannie and Freddie accept VantageScore 4.0; FICO 10T not yet eligible. FHFA put Classic FICO and VantageScore 4.0 on the same loan-level pricing grid.
