HEADLINE RESULTS — Q3 FY2026
============================

Total Revenue: $8,558M (~$8.6B), up 10% YoY (Q3 FY25: ~$7,780M)
  vs. consensus estimate: ~$8,530-$8,540M → beat by ~$20-28M (~+0.3-0.7%)

GAAP Operating Income: ~$4,000M (up 8% YoY)
Non-GAAP Operating Income: ~$4,700M (up 8% YoY)

GAAP Diluted EPS: $11.09 (up 11% YoY from $10.02)
Non-GAAP Diluted EPS: $12.80 (up 10% YoY from $11.65)
  vs. consensus estimate: $12.28-$12.57 → beat by $0.23-$0.52 (+1.8-4.2%)

CFO quote: "We exceeded the top end of our guidance across revenue, operating income
and earnings per share." — Sandeep Aujla

============================
SEGMENT REVENUES — Q3 FY2026
============================

GLOBAL BUSINESS SOLUTIONS GROUP (GBSG): $3,300M (up 15% YoY)
  - Online Ecosystem Revenue: $2,500M (up 19% YoY; +22% ex-Mailchimp)
    - QBO Advanced + Intuit Enterprise Suite combined: ~38% growth
    - QBO Online Accounting revenue: +22% (driven by higher effective prices, customer growth, mix shift)
    - International online revenue: +10% constant currency
    - Mailchimp: revenue declined slightly YoY
  - GBSG ex-Mailchimp: +17%

CONSUMER GROUP: $5,300M (up 8% YoY)
  - TurboTax Total: $4,400M (up 7% YoY)
    - TurboTax Live: representing ~53% of total TurboTax revenue (up ~11 pts YoY)
    - TurboTax Live revenue growth: ~36% for full year
    - TurboTax Live customers: ~38% growth for full year; new customers up 29%
    - TurboTax Online paying units: +2% (ARPU +11%, driven by assisted tier mix, fast refunds)
    - Total TurboTax online units: ~-2% (pay-nothing customers: ~7M, down from ~8M)
    - TurboTax e-file share: down ~1 point
    - Fast Money refunds expected: ~$25B
    - Credit Karma tax filers starting in platform: +54%
  - Credit Karma: $631M (up 15% YoY; personal loans contributed +9 ppts of growth)
  - ProTax: $278M (flat YoY)

[No separate GBSG/Consumer segment subtotals are publicly broken out for QBO ARPU —
only "QBO Online Accounting revenue +22%" is disclosed. QBO Online ARPU not separately
reported; inferred improvement from pricing actions and mix shift per management commentary.]

FULL-YEAR FY2026 GUIDANCE (RAISED)
============================

Prior guidance (as of Q2 FY26): Revenue $20.997B–$21.186B; non-GAAP EPS $22.98–$23.18
Raised guidance (Q3 FY26):
  Total revenue: $21.341B–$21.374B (13-14% growth) — raised from prior midpoint ~$21.09B
  GAAP operating income: $5.705B–$5.725B (~16% growth)
  Non-GAAP operating income: $8.784B–$8.804B (~16% growth)
  GAAP diluted EPS: $15.79–$15.84 (~16% growth; includes ~$300M restructuring charges)
  Non-GAAP diluted EPS: $23.80–$23.85 (~18% growth) — raised from prior $22.98–$23.18

Segment guidance (FY2026 full year):
  GBSG: ~16% growth
  Consumer: ~10% growth
    TurboTax: ~7% growth → $5.277B–$5.282B (revised down from $5.305B–$5.330B)
    Credit Karma: ~19% growth
    ProTax: ~4% growth

TurboTax full-year detailed:
  TurboTax Live revenue: ~36% growth to $2.800B (~53% of total TurboTax)
  TurboTax Live customers: ~38% growth
  TurboTax online paying units: ~+2%; ARPU ~+11%
  Total TurboTax online units: ~-2%
  TurboTax e-file share: -~1 point

Q4 FY2026 guidance:
  Revenue growth: 11-12%
  Non-GAAP diluted EPS: $3.56–$3.62

============================
WORKFORCE RESTRUCTURING
============================

Announcement: 17% full-time workforce reduction (~3,000+ employees of ~18,200 FTE)
Effective: Final day July 31, 2026 for US employees
Severance: 16 weeks base pay + 2 weeks per year of tenure
Offices closing: Reno, NV and Woodland Hills, CA (consolidating to key hubs)
Restructuring charges: $300M–$340M (primarily Q4 FY2026)
Rationale per Goodarzi: "reduce management layers," eliminate "coordination-heavy roles,"
  become "faster, leaner and more focused"
CEO statement: "Nothing to do with AI" (referring to why the cuts are not AI replacement)

============================
IRS DIRECT FILE STATUS
============================

IRS Direct File program was terminated by the Trump administration in late 2025.
Program NOT available for 2026 filing season.
Management did NOT discuss Direct File on the Q3 FY26 earnings call.
TurboTax DIY pressure attributed to PRICE SENSITIVITY among sub-$50K income filers,
not IRS Direct File cannibalization. Management is pivoting to value-based pricing.
