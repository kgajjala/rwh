=== ACCOUNTING / SEGMENT REPORTING CHANGES ===
1. Beginning fiscal 2027, share-based compensation expense is NO LONGER EXCLUDED from
   non-GAAP financial measures. Company rationale: SBC is "a recurring component of our
   compensation program." FY27 guidance embeds a $5.81/share SBC impact.
   Stated targets: SBC to fall to 9% of revenue by FY2028 and 8% by FY2030.
2. Effective August 1, 2026, Mailchimp is managed as a separate operating segment from
   Global Business Solutions and becomes a separate reportable segment in fiscal 2027.
3. A full recast of segment margins for FY2024–FY2026 will be provided at Investor Day
   on September 17, 2026.
