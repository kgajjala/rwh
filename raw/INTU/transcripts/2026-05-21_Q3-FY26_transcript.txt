============================
SASAN GOODARZI (CEO) — KEY VERBATIM QUOTES
============================

ON AI MOAT / COMPETITIVE DIFFERENTIATION:
"Our powerful combination of proprietary data, domain-specific AI platform capabilities,
and AI-powered human expertise is setting the standard for trusted financial intelligence."

"Customers buy confidence not code. Which is why they spend at least 7x more on
accounting and tax experts than on software alone."

"The majority of people are buying confidence to get assistance, and it is irrespective
of how much software improves."

"The money that is spent on tax experts has actually gone up in the last 5 years,
no matter how good technology becomes."

ON TURBOTAX DIY PRESSURE / UNDER-$50K STRATEGY:
"We faced pressure among the most price sensitive DIY filers earning less than
$50 thousand a year. We lost on price."

"We need a durable approach, to winning with these customers that are again less
than $50 thousand in income."

"The shift from complexity based to value based...if you earn less than $50 thousand
and you have a W-2, you may fall into a SKU that is free."

"We will evolve our business model by delivering the right lineup and price points."

ON WORKFORCE REDUCTION:
"We are reducing our full time workforce by 17% to simplify our organizational structure
to become a faster, leaner and more focused company."

"The first is we significantly reduced the number of management layers."

"The second...reducing the number of what we call coordination heavy roles."

"A big chunk of this, you can count on it to go to margin expansion and EPS growth."

"This is all about the 3 Fs: focused organization, flatter organization, faster organization."

[Note: CEO explicitly stated the workforce reduction "had nothing to do with AI"
in terms of AI replacing workers — the cuts were organizational simplification.
Source: CNBC headline https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/20/intuit-ceo-says-companys-17percent-workforce-cut-had-nothing-to-do-with-ai.html]

ON INTUIT ENTERPRISE SUITE (IES) / QBO ADVANCED:
"Online ecosystem revenue for QBO Advanced and Intuit Enterprise Suite grew approximately 38%."

"37% quarter over quarter growth of total Intuit Enterprise Suite contracts."

"We are scaling our direct sales team by approximately 30% as we shared last quarter
and solid productivity continues to improve."

"The system of intelligence that we have created as a control tower to help businesses
and accountants run their business...is all driven by data and AI."

ON TURBOTAX LIVE:
"TurboTax Live customers to grow 38% this year...representing over half of TurboTax
revenue, up 11-points versus last year, a significant milestone."

ON MAILCHIMP:
"Mailchimp revenue declined slightly year over year, prompting organizational rightsizing."

============================
SANDEEP AUJLA (CFO) — KEY VERBATIM QUOTES
============================

ON FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE:
"We exceeded the top end of our guidance across revenue, operating income and earnings per share."

ON QBO ONLINE ACCOUNTING:
"QuickBooks Online Accounting revenue grew 22%, driven by higher effective prices,
customer growth, and mix shift."

ON SUB-$50K TURBOTAX CLARIFICATION:
"In the sub-50k segment, there are millions of customers that we serve exceptionally
well...What Sasan is referring to is the price sensitive segment of the under-$50k,
which is a segment of the under-$50k, not the entirety."

ON MAILCHIMP STRATEGIC VALUE:
"The Mailchimp provides cash flow profile will generate more value for Intuit than a
third party is likely to pay for that asset in the current equity and debt environment
for software."

[Implies Mailchimp sale was evaluated and rejected — management views it as a
profitable cash-flow asset to run for profitability rather than divest at current
software valuations.]

ON LONG-TERM EPS GROWTH:
"We are committed to delivering annual EPS growth of at least mid-teens over the
coming years."

ON FY2027 PRELIMINARY GUIDANCE (verbatim from Globe and Mail transcript):
"Revenue growth of 11%-12%; GAAP EPS growth of $0.73-$0.79; non-GAAP EPS growth
of $3.56-$3.62."

[These $3.56-$3.62 and $0.73-$0.79 figures are DOLLAR INCREMENT GROWTH, not absolute EPS.
Implied FY2027 non-GAAP EPS ≈ $27.36-$27.47 ($23.80-$23.85 FY26 base + $3.56-$3.62 growth).]

ON COST REDUCTION FLOW-THROUGH:
"The majority of cost reductions we do expect to flow to the bottom line."
