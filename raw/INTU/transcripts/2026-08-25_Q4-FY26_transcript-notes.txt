=== SASAN GOODARZI (CEO) — TURBOTAX / DIY PRICING RESET ===
"Price is now the number one reason customers leave TurboTax."
"We lost quality DIY customers to lower cost providers this year."
Company is "deliberately accepting lower initial DIY tax ARPC to acquire and retain more
quality customers."
On distribution: "expanding the distribution so customers can discover and access TurboTax
where they already are, including through leading LLM experiences" and new payroll
provider partnerships.
Accountability: "I am personally dissatisfied and hold myself accountable for the lack of
performance, which is DIY tax, and on the low end in the business group."

=== GOODARZI — FRAMING THE FY27 RESET ===
"We are resetting expectations for the company because this is the perfect time to do it
where we can play offense."
"We are creating the pressure. We are not being pressured to make the change."
"AI will be a disruptor, and we intend to be the disruptor."
Positioning: "a unified financial system of intelligence... increasingly does the work for
our customers."
Model: "acquire more new customers earlier, and grow with them as their needs become more
complex."

=== CUSTOMER / SEGMENT METRICS DISCLOSED ON THE CALL ===
QuickBooks total online paying customers: 8.9M at FY26 end, +3% YoY
  (roughly 2 points slower than the prior year)
QuickBooks Online Accounting revenue: +23% for the full year
QuickBooks Free/Lite: ">20,000 customers either actively using QuickBooks Free or who had
  converted to paid offerings" as of last month
Mid-market revenue: +39% in Q4; mid-market customers +28%, "roughly three-quarters of the
  additions coming from upgrades or desktop migrations"
New-to-franchise mid-market customers: +30% for the year
Intuit Enterprise Suite (IES): annualized revenue "surpassed $145 million in Q4, a 4x
  increase from last year"; "over 75% of Intuit Enterprise Suite customers use our AI
  agents every month"
Desktop Ecosystem: ~25% of GBS; expected low-single-digit decline
Credit Karma: +16% in Q4, +20% full year; FY27 guided +11–13%
Cross-sell: "Customers using both TurboTax and Credit Karma generate approximately twice
  the average revenue per customer"; >50% growth in Credit Karma members filing with
  TurboTax in FY26
TurboTax US units: 39.0M, -2% YoY
Mailchimp: separate reportable segment beginning FY27; FY27 revenue "flat to down 1%";
  "focused on maximizing its value and delivering strong profitability"

=== SANDEEP AUJLA (CFO) — NON-GAAP DEFINITION CHANGE ===
"share-based compensation expenses will no longer be excluded from our non-GAAP financial
measures... a recurring component of our compensation program."
FY27 non-GAAP diluted EPS "$22.88 to $23.12, or growth of 23%-24%," inclusive of a
"$5.81 impact from share-based compensation expenses."
SBC targets: 9% of revenue by FY2028; "we now expect share-based compensation as a
percentage of revenue to be 8% by fiscal 2030."
Margin: "We expect margin expansion driven by savings from workforce changes... partially
offset by deliberate choices... to increase investments in areas such as sales and
marketing."

=== CAPITAL ALLOCATION ===
"$5.5 billion of stock repurchased during the year, up 96% versus last year"
"$2.1 billion of stock repurchased during the fourth quarter, an increase of 179%"
"quarterly dividend of $1.38 per share... a 15% increase versus last year"
Remaining buyback authorization: $7.9B

=== LONG-TERM GROWTH ALGORITHM (RESET) ===
Global Business Solutions: 10%-15% CAGR "over the next three years"
  (Barclays' Raimo Lenschau flagged this as a cut from the prior 15%-20% framing; Aujla
   attributed the reduction to the Desktop Ecosystem headwind, ~25% of GBS)
Consumer: 4%-8% CAGR over the next three years
Company aspiration: "our goal is to be a durable double-digit revenue growth company over
the long term"
On growth levers: "we are being prudent in the contribution we are seeing from each
[volume, mix, price] in our long-term expectations"

=== ANALYST Q&A THEMES ===
Brad Zelnick (Deutsche Bank): Is FY27 the bottom of a J-curve or a structural shift from AI
  competition? Goodarzi defended the reset as offense, citing prior periods when a
  focused share strategy delivered double-digit DIY customer growth and 20%+ business growth.
Raimo Lenschau (Barclays): Pressed on the GBS long-term guide cut from 15%-20% to 10%-15%.
Arjun Bhatia (William Blair): Could re-acceleration slip to FY2028? Goodarzi declined to
  commit to a timeline, preferring quarterly execution.
Karl Keirstead / UBS (McGinnis): Probed the customer-growth slowdown; Goodarzi cited ~7%
  TAM penetration and QuickBooks Free/Lite proof points.
Siti Panigrahi (Mizuho): Asked whether guidance embeds conservatism vs. real market pressure.
