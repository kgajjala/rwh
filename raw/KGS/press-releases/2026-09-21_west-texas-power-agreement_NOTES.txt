Extracted facts (full text not reproducible through the fetch tool; facts cross-checked across the four sources above):
- 76 MW of behind-the-meter baseload power, including balance of plant, for a West Texas data center.
- Term: six years.
- Equipment: approximately 40 natural-gas-fueled reciprocating generation units from Kodiak's existing fleet (CEO: the size "fits our available power fleet").
- Customer: a data center operator with an investment-grade hyperscaler under contract; a GPU designer guarantees the data center lease.
- Deployment begins Q4 2026, scaling into Q1 2027; revenue recognition expected to begin Q1 2027.
- CEO Mickey McKee: Kodiak's second long-term contract to supply primary power to a data center; about half of the current power portfolio is now under long-term contracts.
- Contract value and pricing: not disclosed.
- Stock reaction: −1.6% to $57.77 on Sep 21 (Baystreet); $52.91 by Sep 25 (S-3ASR cover).
Context: on the Q2 2026 call (Aug 7) management described its West Texas LNTP project as "sub-100-megawatt", supplying power "in early 2027", for a data center "whose capacity is leased out to a hyperscaler" — the Sep 21 contract matches on every disclosed attribute [Derived].
