Kodiak Gas Services, Inc. (KGS +1.23%) reported record financial results for the second quarter of 2026, supported by high utilization in its compression business and the formal launch of its power infrastructure segment. Management increased full-year guidance for adjusted EBITDA and discretionary cash flow, reflecting operational efficiencies and visibility into natural gas production growth. The company secured a major turbine supply agreement to support its goal of 2 gigawatts of power generation capacity by 2030 and initiated engineering work on its first significant data center project in West Texas.
