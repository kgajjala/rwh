Compression: utilization 98.3%; revenue-generating hp 4.5M (+~102K QoQ incl. 43K hp bought out of operating leases); revenue per ending hp $23.51, +3% YoY, down sequentially, "just a shade over $24" excluding lease buyouts and one terminated contract (McKee/Griggs); adjusted gross margin 70% (+170 bps YoY); FY26 compression margin guide 69.5-70.5%; ~170K hp additions in 2026; CAT 3600 lead times ~190 weeks; lube oil locked for 2027.
Power: revenue $42M (+28% QoQ); adjusted gross margin 75% (64.5% in Q2); fleet 413 MW, 382 revenue-generating, 92.5% utilization; 16 MW recips due Q4, 29 MW for the year; 2026 power growth capex $350-400M; Baker Hughes 1.1 GW through 2031 (>$1.4B capital, Griggs). West Texas: 40 recips, startup Q1 2027.
Contract terms (Griggs): projects "penciling in at high teens or better with ... five- and six-year payback"; (McKee) "a minimum of 10-year terms, most of them at 15-year terms." No MW-month price disclosed.
Balance sheet (Griggs): leverage 3.1x, total debt ~$2.8B, ~$1.6B undrawn revolver; target 3-4x; May equity raise ~$836M net "pre-funding our equity needs." No buyback discussed. FY2027 capex and guidance deferred to the Q4 call.
FY2026 adjusted EBITDA guide midpoint raised $5M to $850M.
Q&A: J.P. Morgan (Eli Johnson) on a post-election shift in Texas; McKee: "there is some, a little bit of uncertainty in Texas." Wells Fargo (Praneet Satish) noted softening language on water, noise, emissions rules. Jefferies pressed on whether Texas stays the majority location; McKee: "a lot of water to go under the bridge." No moratorium discussed. "A couple hundred megawatts that we have to pull off of legacy contracts" (McKee).
Stock: StockStory reports shares +9.2% to $45.50 after the print.
