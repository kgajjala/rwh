# 8-K, Nov 12, 2026 — CEO transition (summary extract)

Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000082/nvidiaannouncesceotransiti.htm

- Jensen Huang resigned as President, CEO and director, effective immediately.
- Colette Kress (EVP and CFO) is Interim CEO until a permanent successor is named, continuing as CFO.
- Board "initiating a search for a permanent Chief Executive Officer", internal and external candidates.
- Board chair Harvey Jones expects operations, customer commitments and roadmap "to continue without interruption".
- No financial guidance; Q3 FY2027 results on Nov 17 as scheduled. No separation terms disclosed.
