# NVIDIA FY2026 10-K (fiscal year ended Jan 25, 2026) — summary extract

Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/nvda-20260125.htm (read 2026-11-17 via WebFetch summaries; quotes below are short verbatim fragments returned by the reader)

- Competitors named (Item 1): GPUs/accelerators AMD, Huawei, Intel; cloud/internal designs Alibaba, Alphabet, Amazon, Baidu, Huawei, Microsoft; Arm CPUs Amazon, Huawei, Microsoft; networking AMD, Arista, Broadcom, Cisco, HPE, Huawei, Intel, Lumentum, Marvell.
- Customers: two direct customers 22% and 14% of FY2026 revenue (FY2025: 12%, 11%, 11%). Some indirect customers individually ≥10%.
- Revenue from customers headquartered outside the US: 31% (FY2025: 41%).
- Data Center revenue +68%; compute +59% (Blackwell); networking +142% (NVLink for GB200/GB300, Ethernet and InfiniBand).
- Segment operating income: Compute & Networking $130,141M (FY2025 $82,875M); Graphics $9,156M ($5,085M).
- Buybacks FY2026: 282M shares, $40.4B; remaining authorization $58.5B at Jan 25, 2026. Dividends $974M. OCF $102,718M. SBC $6,386M.
- Purchase and long-term supply/capacity obligations: $95.2B at Jan 25, 2026.
- Risk factors (fragments): customers "can use or develop their own solutions to replace those we are providing"; "Our target markets remain competitive, and competition may intensify"; "we were effectively foreclosed from competing in China's data center computing/compute market"; supply risks "have increased and may continue to increase as our purchase obligations and prepaids have grown"; Groq IP license "required significant, nonrefundable payments".
- $4.5B charge in Q1 FY2026 for H20 excess inventory and purchase obligations.
- ~42,000 employees in 38 countries. 24.3B shares outstanding at Feb 20, 2026.
