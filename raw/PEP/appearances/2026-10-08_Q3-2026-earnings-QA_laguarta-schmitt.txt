Points:
- FY2026 guide cut: core constant-currency EPS +1% to +2% (was low end of +4% to +6%); core EPS +2.5% to +3.5%; organic revenue ~+3% (was +2% to +4%); core tax rate ~21%; cash returns unchanged at $8.9B ($7.9B dividends, $1.0B buybacks).
- North America convenient foods (PFNA) core operating margin down 280 bps in Q3 (Laguarta, per Just Food): affordability investment, lapping a prior-year asset-sale gain, higher A&M. PBNA core margin -15 bps on volume/channel mix and A&M.
- Laguarta: NA core operating margin expected to remain under pressure in Q4, partly offset by productivity.
- Schmitt (per Stocktwits): improving NA growth and margins is "taking more time than we planned"; rising input costs.
- Additional structural cost reductions (corporate costs, discretionary spend, non-growth initiatives) to be implemented in coming months, funding growth investment and offsetting input-cost inflation.
- International organic revenue +8%, fastest since Q1 2024 (Laguarta); international margin +105 bps (secondary, AI-generated summary).
- Q3 core operating profit +3% included a 4-point benefit from tariff refunds (8-K).
- No quantified 2027 commentary in the write-ups reviewed. No update on the Elliott-committed North America supply-chain review in the write-ups reviewed.
