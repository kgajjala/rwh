## FY2026 shareholder letter (ARS filed 2026-08-28) — Pattern C, new in window
"We met our core objectives for fiscal year 2026 despite unexpected headwinds."
"Organic sales grew more than 1%. Core earnings per share grew 1%. On a currency neutral basis,
 core earnings per share were in-line with the prior year."
"Over the course of the year, we sequentially improved top-line results — moving from up one point
 in the first half of the year to two points in the second half."
"Nine of 10 product categories held or grew organic sales for the year... Family Care was down."
"Twenty-six of our top 50 category/country combinations held or grew share for the fiscal year.
 Five of 10 product categories held or grew share globally. In aggregate, global value and volume
 share trends improved in the back-half, exiting the year roughly flat."
"E-commerce sales increased 6%, now representing 20% of Company total sales."
"There is clearly more work to be done to sustain and accelerate the momentum underway."
