Rivian Releases Q2 2026 Production and Delivery Figures, Raises Full Year Delivery Outlook (Irvine, CA, July 2, 2026)
Source: 8-K Ex. 99.1, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1874178/000187417826000048/ex-9912q26deliveryproducti.htm
Captured 2026-10-19.

- Q2 2026 production 12,613; deliveries 12,194 (Normal, IL).
- Deliveries topped the company's Q2 outlook of 9,000-11,000, "due to robust growth quarter-over-quarter in EDV and R1 coupled with the introduction of R2 deliveries."
- FY2026 delivery guidance raised in this release from 62,000-67,000 to 65,000-70,000.
- Q2 results set for July 30, 2026 after market close.
Note: the guidance change was made in the delivery release, not on the earnings call.
