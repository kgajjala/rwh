6M 2026 net loss includes a $506M gain on the Mind Robotics Series A / deconsolidation.
Q2 automotive gross profit includes ~$100M of incremental R2 ramp costs (expedited freight, supplier premiums, unabsorbed fixed cost).

Balance sheet Jun 30, 2026: cash + STI $5,310M; ABL availability $536M; long-term debt $4,444M. Pro-forma liquidity $7,163M incl. ~$1,317M net from the July equity offering.
2026 guidance: deliveries 65-70K; adjusted EBITDA $(2.0)-(1.8)B (midpoint +$50M); capex $1.70-1.80B (midpoint -$250M).
External R2 deliveries began June 9, 2026. Two R2 shifts targeted by end of Q3, volume mainly Q4.
VW: $1.0B non-recourse loan (Oct 2026, at Rivian's option); $460M equity at earlier of first RV Tech vehicle SOP or Jan 2028.
Uber: $250M milestone-based investment targeted by end of 2026; up to $700M more through 2031 on autonomy milestones.
Call (secondary): CFO said $164M of H1 regulatory credits benefited gross profit and "largely" fall away in H2 — "the bigger driver" of a steeper H2 EBITDA loss (247wallst 2026-08-17). Q3 to carry a full quarter of R2 ramp cost.
