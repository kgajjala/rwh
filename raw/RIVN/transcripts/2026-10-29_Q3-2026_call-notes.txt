NOTES (not a verbatim copy) on the Rivian Q3 2026 earnings call, October 29, 2026
Sources: Q&A read in full in the browser from https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/30/rivian-rivn-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/ (Motley Fool; the page begins at the first analyst question, so the prepared remarks were NOT retrieved); prepared-remark points from the MarketBeat summary https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/transcript-what-rivian-automotive-rivn-said-on-its-q3-earnings-call-2026-10-29/ (secondary).
Speakers: RJ Scaringe (founder and CEO), Derek Mulvey (VP finance, interim CFO from Oct 30), Claire McDonough (outgoing CFO), Javier Varela (operations)

- Mulvey, answering whether Q4 will show a full quarter of positive automotive gross profit: "we intend to exit the quarter at an Automotive gross profit. So I wouldn't expect the full quarter, but it does set us up really well as we continue to ramp R2 and ... the progression we see through 2027."
- Mulvey on the ~$120M of incremental ramp cost: it compares today's conversion cost and depreciation with a two-shift operation, and includes expedited freight to wind off as production scales. "In the second quarter, we saw about a $12,000 per unit step down in cost of goods sold per unit delivered ... Automotive gross profit, excluding regulatory credits, on a cash basis, that was positive for the quarter."
- Scaringe on commodity inflation (memory especially): the team is finding offsets to keep trims at "the base trim starting at $45,000 and the top spec trim being at about $57,000"; some improvements will arrive with the Georgia plant (Stanton Springs), others flow into vehicles "over the coming months and quarters".
- Scaringe on R2 ramp: "we can only ramp as fast as our slowest ramping supplier." Varela: hundreds of suppliers with Tier 2 and Tier 3 behind them.
- Autonomy+: take rate "has exceeded our expectations"; the attach rate is not disclosed. Point to Point is "an important capability"; "there's really only one other product that has that, of course, with Tesla's FSD".
- EDV vans: Amazon "double down to continue to buy more vehicles"; other commercial operators are looking. Volkswagen JV: first output, the VW ID.1 (a roughly EUR 20,000 EV for Europe), launches next year.
- MarketBeat summary: delivery guide raised to 67,000-70,000; R2 expected to be a majority of Q4 deliveries; the $300M capex cut is mainly timing, including Georgia; $5.3B of the $5.8B of VW JV commitments received, $460M expected in late 2027; the second Uber milestone expected by year-end would trigger $250M of equity; CFO McDonough steps down and Mulvey becomes interim CFO.
