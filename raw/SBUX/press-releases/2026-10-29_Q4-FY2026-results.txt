Q4 FY2026 (13 weeks ended Sep 27, 2026) vs Q4 FY2025
- Global comparable coffeehouse sales +7.2% (ticket +5.2%, transactions +1.8%). North America +7.9% (ticket +5.0%, transactions +2.7%); U.S. +7.7% (ticket +5.2%, transactions +2.4%); International +0.9% (ticket +3.7%, transactions -2.8%).
- Net revenues $9,141.2M (-4.5%; China moved to a licensed JV in Q3). Net new coffeehouses 303; 41,607 total (32% company-operated, 68% licensed); U.S. 16,781; ~250 North America company-operated stores closed in September.
- GAAP operating income $991.8M, margin 10.8% (+790 bps); non-GAAP operating margin 14.0% (+450-460 bps). GAAP EPS $0.59 (vs $0.12); non-GAAP EPS $0.80 (+54%). Tax rate 27.1% GAAP, 24.7% non-GAAP. Diluted shares 1,144.6M.
- North America: revenue $7,328.4M (+6%), operating income $898.4M, margin 12.3% (4.5%); restructuring $270.2M. International: revenue $1,305.8M (-37%), operating income $256.9M, margin 19.7%. Channel Development: revenue $494.4M, operating income $264.3M, margin 53.5%.
- Dividend $0.63 a quarter (66 consecutive quarters), payable Nov 27, 2026; CFO: "plans to resume share repurchases in fiscal 2027".
