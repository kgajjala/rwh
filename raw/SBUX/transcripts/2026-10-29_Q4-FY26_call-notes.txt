NOTES (not a verbatim copy) on the Starbucks Q4 FY2026 earnings call, October 29, 2026
Source read in browser: https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/30/starbucks-sbux-q4-2026-earnings-call-transcript/ (Motley Fool; second source not retrieved)
Speakers: Brian Niccol (Chairman and CEO), Cathy Smith (CFO)

- Niccol: "Back to Starbucks started as a turnaround plan and now Back to Starbucks is our growth plan"; fifth consecutive quarter of global comp growth; "ahead of schedule" on the fiscal 2028 framework; more than 80% of North America company-operated coffeehouses had positive comps (under 50% a year earlier); inventory availability at an all-time high; Rewards 90-day active members a record 36 million.
- Smith: U.S. comps +7.7%, transactions +2.4% and ticket +5.2%; "Contribution from transactions moderated from Q3, particularly as we began to lap the growth"; on a two-year basis U.S. transaction comps improved sequentially. Pricing and mix (including merchandise) drove most of the sequential ticket improvement. North America operating margin about 16% non-GAAP (+440 bps); Green Apron labor investments lapped in August. Interest and other income of $59.2M included net investment gains. Q4 tax rate 24.7%.
- FY2027 guidance (Smith): revenue about -1% (including ~$700M from the 53rd week); North America comps ~+4%, "led by beverage and food innovation, Green Apron Service, delivery and a small contribution from uplifts and sales transfer from coffeehouse closures"; "Any pricing will remain focused and well below CPI"; consolidated operating margin 13.5%-14% (+190 bps at the midpoint); G&A flat to slightly down; EPS $3.15-3.25 (about +18% at the midpoint, incl. slightly more than $0.10 from the 53rd week); ~1,200 net new coffeehouses (about 1,100 international, China about half; North America 90-100 net new, about half licensed). Margin cadence: a little better than 40-45% of the year's earnings in the first half. Channel Development revenue about -10%.
- Savings program: over 50% of the $2B gross savings achieved; more cost-of-goods savings in FY2027-28. Niccol: "we targeted the idea of 13.5% to 15% margins by fiscal year '28 ... we are already believing we will achieve that low end ... in fiscal year '27."
- Capital allocation: priorities are investing, an investment-grade balance sheet and a competitive dividend (quarterly dividend raised to $0.63, 16th consecutive annual increase); "approximately $1 billion towards share repurchases" in FY2027; capex about 5% of net revenue.
- First-quarter FY2027 call tentatively Wednesday, January 27, 2027.
