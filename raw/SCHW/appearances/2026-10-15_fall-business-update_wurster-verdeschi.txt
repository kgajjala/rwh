GUIDANCE (Verdeschi, prepared remarks)
 - Full-year revenue and NIM expected "towards the lower end of the ranges" given at the Summer Business Update (revenue +17.5% to +18.5%; NIM 3.00% to 3.10%). Expenses also at the low end of the range. Cause named: "loan growth has outpaced deposit growth, which has resulted in greater utilization of our diversified funding sources."
 - About 800 bps of positive operating leverage in 2026, "roughly double what we expected at the beginning of the year."
 - 2027: strong momentum entering the year; detailed 2027 financial scenario at the Winter Business Update in January 2027.
 - Wurster: the same elements "underpin the financial framework we outlined at Investor Day to deliver mid-teens earnings growth through the cycle."
NIM (Q&A, Blostein / Verdeschi)
 - Q3 NIM 3.04%. Q4: "roughly 25 to 30 basis points of NIM expansion that year over year for 4Q25 to 4Q26," and further expansion in 2027. Q4 2025 NIM was 2.90% (Jan 21 release), so the implied Q4 2026 NIM is about 3.15% to 3.20%, against the 3.25% to 3.30% given in July. The analyst's question referred to the "325 to 330" prior guide; the CFO did not repeat it.
 - Lending balances now exceed the securities portfolio. About $190B of securities yield under 2%; "even if we deployed it into securities relative to where those yields are today, that would be enormously accretive." "I do think about net interest margin as Fed funds plus."
 - Rate sensitivity unchanged: the "$250 million to $300 million sensitivity that we include in our materials." Floating-rate loans partly swapped to fixed to match deposits.
CASH (Verdeschi, Q&A Blostein and Chubak)
 - Sweep cash $492.8B, +$7.1B q/q, "reflecting an increase in the long/short strategy and organic growth, partially offset by clients reinvesting in markets given improving sentiment."
 - "Even with those higher rates, this is not cash sorting in any way." Cash went back to the market on sentiment and IPO activity. Seasonal inflow expected from mid-November.
 - Chubak noted markets price three more hikes through 2027.
CAPITAL (Verdeschi; Katz Q&A)
 - Adjusted Tier 1 leverage 7.0%, top of the 6.75% to 7.00% range, "as we allowed capital to build to support continued strong client activity against a backdrop of volatile rates." Nine-month repurchases "almost $4 billion." Q3 repurchase $0.5B (4.7M shares, release).
 - Priority order unchanged: capital to support clients first, then "opportunistically return capital across multiple forms." On pace of repurchase vs. earning-asset growth, no number given; "flexibility to return capital."
GROWTH (Wurster)
 - Core NNA $190.4B (+38% y/y, 5.8% annualized). Retail NNA +39% y/y; Advisor Services NNA continued "no deceleration" after growth in long/short was "throttled" in mid-September (minimum raised; see InvestmentNews/Bloomberg). Wurster: a competitor "had less availability to support this program, and so our growth accelerated at a path we likely did not anticipate."
 - Plans a proprietary long/short product built on direct indexing; no launch date.
 - Managed investing net flows $24B (record); Schwab Wealth Advisory flows +55%; advice penetration ~5% of retail households vs. 31% willing to pay. Schwab ~2% of the $37T U.S. advice market.
 - Retail households with a dedicated financial consultant: ~3x the NNA, ~5x the managed investing flows.
AI (Wurster, Worthington Q&A)
 - Schwab blocks unauthorized agents logging in for clients; allows clients' LLMs to connect to its MCP servers under Schwab authentication with no money movement; first RIA custodian in Claude for Financial Advisors; "Charlie" AI assistant for all clients in coming months.
 - LLM/API trading clients "doubled in about the last six months"; that group "holds more cash than other active traders." Developer productivity up ~25%. "AI has been an enormous accelerant for the company."
OTHER
 - Prediction markets: binary options on the S&P now; financial events and company KPIs "over the next 12 to 18 months" with Cboe; no sports. Crypto: transfers rolling out, three new coins by year-end. Extended-hours trading: 1% to 2% of activity, limited uptake. Will not build quantitative trading strategies for clients.
 - Monetization (Ryan Q&A): ETF monetization, crypto, prediction markets; cash strategy unchanged.
