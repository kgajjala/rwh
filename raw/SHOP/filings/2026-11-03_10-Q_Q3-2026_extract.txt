MD&A — overview
- 9M revenue $10.5B, +33%. Subscription 23% of revenue (25%). Merchant 77% (75%).
- Subscription 9M $2.0B -> $2.4B (+21%). MRR $230M at Sep 30, +19% vs $193M.
- "In the three months ended September 30, 2026, the MRR growth rate for the period was consistent with the same period in 2025."
- Merchant solutions 9M $5.9B -> $8.1B (+37%).
- GMV Q3 $119.1B, 9M $335.5B (Q3 2025 $92.0B, 9M $254.6B): +29% quarter, +32% YTD (2025 vs 2024: 32% and 29%). Constant currency +30% and +30% (2025: 30% and 28%).
- Shopify Payments: "For the three months ended September 30, 2026, the Shopify Payments penetration rate was 68%, resulting in GMV of $81.4 billion that was facilitated using Shopify Payments. This compares to a penetration rate of 65% resulting in GMV of $60.2 billion ... in the same period in 2025." Payments GMV +$21.3B, +35%. Shopify Payments revenue +$619M in Q3. 9M: penetration 68% (65%), $226.5B ($164.3B), +38%.
- Mix: "Gross profit margins on Shopify Payments ... are typically lower than on subscription solutions ... We view Shopify Payments as beneficial to our operating margins, as it requires significantly less sales and marketing and research and development expenses ... continued growth of merchant solutions has caused in the past, and may cause in the future, a decline in our overall gross margin percentage."

Cost of revenues (Q3): subscription 175 (128), +37%: "driven by a $40 million increase in our infrastructure costs, including cloud hosting platforms, infrastructure software and LLM usage costs." 9M +$99M on the same.
Gross profit Q3 1,781 (1,391), +28%; 48% of revenue (49%). 9M 5,035 (3,862), +30%.

Opex (Q3 / Q3 2025):
- S&M 466 / 410 (+14%), 12% of revenue (14%): +$26M marketing programs, +$21M affiliate payouts.
- R&D 497 / 375 (+33%), 13% (13%): +$62M employee costs "including timing of product development events" and +$40M computer hardware and software "which includes AI-related usage".
- G&A 130 / 115 (+13%).
- Transaction and loan losses 120 / 148 (−19%): "$41 million decrease in losses related to Shopify Payments driven by lower realized losses ... offset by a $14 million increase in losses related to lending services driven by an expansion of our offerings". 9M 377 / 303 (+24%), lending +$83M.
- Operating income 568 (343). 9M 1,438 (837).

Equity investments: Q3 net loss on equity and other investments $127M incl. $126M unrealized loss on listed holdings; 9M $530M gross unrealized gain on "an observable price change of a private investment", $404M unrealized loss on listed holdings. Equity method loss $21M Q3.
Tax: released $63M of US valuation allowance as a discrete benefit: "The main positive evidence was sustained profits of its U.S. operations and projections of future taxable income."
