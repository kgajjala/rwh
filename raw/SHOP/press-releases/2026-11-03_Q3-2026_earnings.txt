HEADLINE: Shopify Sustains Rapid Growth with Revenue Up 32% and Free Cash Flow Margin of 22%

"Shopify is uncommonly good at operating in two realities at the same time: the world our merchants exist in today, and the one they are moving towards. Q3 is the perfect example. We powered $119 billion in GMV, while continuing to build commerce into every surface, every agent, and increasingly, everywhere," said Harley Finkelstein, President of Shopify.
"We extended our track record of consistent execution, with 32% revenue growth and a 22% free cash flow margin," said Jeff Hoffmeister, Chief Financial Officer of Shopify. "Delivering these results at our scale speaks to the strength of our model, our multiple growth drivers, and our expanding addressable market."
