## SUMMARY

**Skydance Corporation** ([SKYD](https://www.fool.com/quote/nyse/skyd/) -5.25%) completed its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery on Oct. 6, 2026, initiating a multiyear transformation aimed at achieving $6 billion in run-rate synergies. Management reported that the integration will focus on consolidating technology stacks, real estate, and corporate overhead while transitioning from legacy media to a next-generation entertainment model. The company stated its intention to prioritize profitable streaming growth and free cash flow generation, targeting a leverage ratio below 3x by the end of 2029. Strategies include bundling flagship streaming services and optimizing a content budget that exceeds $30 billion annually across three primary business segments.

- The company plans to launch a bundle of Paramount+ and HBO Max in early 2027 as a preliminary step toward a fully unified streaming offering.
- Management expects to bring Paramount+ and HBO Max onto the same technology stack within approximately one year, utilizing artificial intelligence tools to accelerate the convergence.
- The $6 billion synergy program includes $2.2 billion in corporate overhead and real estate savings, $1.4 billion in technology reductions, and $1.4 billion from procurement and vendor management.
- Chairman and Chief Executive Officer David Ellison stated, "artificial intelligence is a tool for artists and will never be a replacement for them," while noting its potential to increase productivity and lower production costs.
- The company adopted an annual guidance methodology to emphasize long-term value creation, with the next formal outlook expected in early 2027.
- CFO Dennis Cinelli noted that achieving leverage targets does not require raising additional capital or divesting assets, as the operational plan is designed to fund debt repayment through cash flow.

## INDUSTRY GLOSSARY

- **DTC (Direct-to-Consumer)**: A business model where companies sell products or services directly to customers, bypassing third-party retailers or wholesalers.
- **ARPU (Average Revenue Per User)**: A metric used to track the amount of revenue generated by each customer or subscriber over a specific period.
- **Adjusted EBITDA**: A financial measure that excludes certain non-operating expenses and non-cash charges to assess core operational performance.
- **Pro forma**: A method of calculating financial results that assumes a major event, such as a merger, occurred at the beginning of the period for comparison purposes.
- **Linear Television**: Traditional television broadcasting where viewers watch programs at their scheduled times on a specific channel.
- **Run-rate synergies**: The total annual cost savings expected to be achieved once a transformation or integration program is fully implemented.
- **ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning)**: A software system used by companies to manage and integrate core business processes like finance, HR, and procurement.
