## Launch Operations (2026)
- Falcon 9 2026 cadence guide (Shotwell, Time magazine interview): "maybe 140, 145-ish" launches for the year
- SpaceX alone expected to account for 80–100+ of 250+ total global orbital launches in 2026
- Falcon 9 booster reuse: boosters now flying 20+ missions each — reuse durability has structurally changed satellite-deployment economics
- Falcon Heavy: used for classified NRO missions and DoD payloads too heavy/sensitive for Falcon 9
- Cumulative federal contracts (NASA, DoD, Space Force, NRO): >$22B
- Key government customers: NASA, U.S. Space Force, NRO, DoD
- U.S. government contracts ≈35% of total company revenue (S-1 disclosure); some sources separately cite $10–12B in annual government revenue (broader/different-period estimate — flagged for reconciliation)

## Starship Program
- 11 full-stack test flights completed as of mid-2026
- First Block 3 flight targeted Q2 2026
- 2025–2026 characterized as likely the peak of Falcon-family launch activity, with the company planning to progressively shift missions to Starship over time
- In-orbit refueling: not yet demonstrated (required for lunar/Mars missions and the long-term orbital-compute-satellite plan)
- Artemis lunar architecture role: on the line pending Starship execution
