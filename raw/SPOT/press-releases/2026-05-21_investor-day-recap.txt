MAJOR ANNOUNCEMENTS

1. RESERVED BY SPOTIFY — Concert Ticket Pre-Access
   - Spotify identifies artist's most engaged Premium listeners
   - Reserves up to two concert tickets per superfan before general on-sale
   - No additional fee beyond Premium subscription
   - Launch: Select U.S. tours, summer 2026
   - Partner: Live Nation (multi-year exclusive partnership; Spotify is exclusive audio streaming service for this inventory)
   - Eligibility: U.S. Premium subscribers, 18+

2. SPOTIFY x UMG LICENSING DEAL — Fan-Made AI Covers & Remixes
   - Announced: May 21, 2026
   - Parties: Spotify + Universal Music Group (recorded + publishing)
   - Tool: Paid add-on for Premium subscribers; AI-powered fan-created covers/remixes
   - Framework: Consent (opt-in by artists), Credit, Compensation
   - Revenue: Additional income stream for artists/songwriters; described as margin-neutral or accretive to Spotify
   - Only UMG announced; Sony and WMG not yet signed (described as "first of many")
   - No pricing or launch date disclosed

3. PODCASTS — Memberships
   - New subscription toolset for podcast creators
   - Transcripts, automatic chapters, real-time Q&A features
   - Launch: "Soon"

4. AUDIOBOOKS+
   - On track for $100M annualized recurring revenue (ARR) as of July 2026
   - Higher-hourly add-ons for power readers
   - Family and Student plans launching
   - Spotify for Authors expanding to 10 new languages
   - Audiobook creation tools (with digital voice generation): Beta launch early June 2026
   - Prompted Playlists for Audiobooks: Summer 2026

5. AI / PLATFORM
   - Studio by Spotify Labs: Desktop app for personalized audio generation (Research Preview soon)
   - SongDNA: 265M+ interactions since March 2026 launch
   - Taste Profile & DJ: Expanded with 4 new languages
   - "Large Taste Model" powering personalization
   - Multiple add-on tiers (music, podcasts, audiobooks, AI) rather than single "Music Pro" tier

6. ENGAGEMENT MILESTONE
   - Spotify 20th Anniversary Experience: 100M engagements in 6 days; "single biggest day" of subscriber intake ever
   - Wrapped 2025: 620M shares generated
