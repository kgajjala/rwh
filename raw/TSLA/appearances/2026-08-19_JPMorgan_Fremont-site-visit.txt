NOTE ON STATUS: This is an off-script analyst briefing, not a filed or webcast
disclosure. Several items below are not in any Tesla filing or update letter.

FSD v15
- Described by Tesla as a "step-change," built on seven core technologies.
- Approximately 40% of those technologies already running in the Austin
  robotaxi fleet.
- Early feedback characterized as "encouraging."
- Customer-vehicle release still targeted late 2026 / early 2027, not now.

HARDWARE
- Tesla asserts current HW4 ("AI4") is sufficient for v15 and for unsupervised FSD.
  (Removes the AI5 forced-upgrade overhang for the existing fleet.)
- AI4.5 rolling out: ~10% more compute, roughly double the memory.

OPTIMUS
- Gen 3 design finalized; supply chain "essentially locked in."
- Production at the former Model S/X facility in Fremont.
- EXTERNAL COMMERCIAL SALES could begin "as early as" H2 2027.
- Long-term capacity targets: 1M units/yr Fremont; 10M units/yr Giga Texas.

ROBOTAXI ECONOMICS  *** first per-mile figure Tesla has put to analysts ***
- Long-run cost target of approximately $0.30 per mile for Cybercab operations.
- Fleet expansion anticipated late 2026 into early 2027.
- For contrast: at the October 2024 Cybercab unveil Musk said operating cost
  "is probably going to be around 20 cents a mile" at scale.

SELL-SIDE CONSEQUENCE
JPMorgan's Rajat Gupta took over Tesla coverage from long-time bear Ryan Brinkman
and moved the rating to Neutral with a $445 price target.
