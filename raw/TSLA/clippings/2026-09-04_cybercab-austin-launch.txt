TIMELINE
- 2026-09-03: private launch event outside ACL Live, 2nd Street, Austin. Invited
  shareholders, employees and influencers; guests reportedly signed NDAs.
  No livestream, no stage presentation, no public remarks from Musk.
  Tesla began charging fares the same day.
- 2026-09-04, 5:00 pm local: opened for public booking via the Robotaxi app.

FLEET / GEOGRAPHY
- Launched with "a small number" of vehicles; 45 units subsequently added to the
  fleet per Texas state documents. NHTSA's audit references ~1,000 Cybercabs
  certified.
- Austin robotaxi geofence recently expanded to ~264 square miles.
- Robotaxi network overall: Austin, Dallas, Houston, Miami, Orlando, Tampa,
  6am-10pm daily; plus San Francisco where CA rules still require safety monitors.
- No in-vehicle safety monitors in Cybercabs outside San Francisco.
- Cybercab and converted Model Y robotaxis now operate side by side.

PRICING (launch-window observations, not Tesla disclosure)
- Same route, same time: Model Y Robotaxi $12.10 vs Cybercab $7.77 (~35% cheaper).
- Earlier robotaxi pricing formula observed as $3.00 base + $1.40/mile; after
  launch the Model Y appears to carry a ~$7.00 base while Cybercab kept ~$3.00.
- Launch-day competitive comparison: Montopolis -> ACL Live quoted $19.58 in a
  Robotaxi Model Y vs $12.96 on Uber. Later the same day a 2.5-mile South Congress
  trip quoted $12.15 with a 40-50 min wait vs $7.96 on Uber with <10 min wait.
- Over the following days, including the weekend, Cybercab fares rose materially
  on demand — at times above both Uber and the Model Y robotaxi.

CAVEAT: ride-hail pricing is dynamic; none of the above is a Tesla-disclosed
unit-economics figure. The only cost figure Tesla has given is the ~$0.30/mile
long-run target from the JPMorgan Fremont visit (see appearances/).
