# CMS CY2027 MA rate cycle: advance notice (Jan 26, 2026) to final announcement (Apr 6, 2026) — captured 2026-11-19

Supersedes the date and rate in 2026-04-CMS-2027-MA-Final-Rate-Notice.md (that note recorded "≈+2.0%" and Apr 28; the final announcement was Apr 6 at +2.48%).

Advance notice, Jan 26, 2026 (Healthcare Dive: https://www.healthcaredive.com/news/cms-proposed-2027-advance-notice-chart-reviews-medicare-advantage/810549/):
- Average rate change +0.09% (≈$700M) vs. analyst expectations of roughly 4-6%; CMS actuaries ≈+2.5% including coding trend.
- Proposed: exclude diagnoses from unlinked chart reviews from risk scores (CMS est. >$7B Medicare savings in 2027); recalibrate the risk model on newer FFS data; exclude audio-only telehealth diagnoses.
- Jefferies (Windley): UNH's MA business most exposed per member. Senate Judiciary report cited on UNH coders and in-home visits.
- Post-market Jan 26: HUM −12%, UNH and CVS −9%, ELV −5%. UNH also reported Q4 2025 and its 2026 outlook on Jan 27 (Forbes: https://www.forbes.com/sites/richardmenger/2026/01/27/did-the-trump-administrations-medicare-proposal-cause-unitedhealth-stock-to-drop-20/).

Final announcement, Apr 6, 2026 (CMS fact sheet: https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/2027-medicare-advantage-part-d-rate-announcement; Healthcare Dive: https://www.healthcaredive.com/news/cms-finalizes-higher-medicare-advantage-rates-2027/816633/; Home Health Care News: https://homehealthcarenews.com/2026/04/cms-finalizes-2-48-medicare-advantage-payment-increase-for-2027-higher-than-proposed/):
- +2.48% (>$13B), 4.98% including risk-score trend. Effective growth rate 4.97% -> 5.33% on FFS data through Q4 2025.
- Risk model recalibration DROPPED for 2027. Chart-review exclusion FINALIZED (exception for members switching MA plans).
- Lobbying: Better Medicare Alliance-led campaign (research, ads, signatures, meetings, comments); credited "thousands of beneficiaries" and "hundreds of organizations". TD Cowen: shows industry influence over CMS, and pre-midterm concern about disrupting seniors. Jefferies: relief "hard to overstate". Analysts had expected the final 1-2 points above the advance; it came ≈2.4 points above.
- Medicare Director Chris Klomp: "We're not throwing the risk adjustment model out."
- Critics: Center for Medicare Advocacy, "CMS Caves to Medicare Advantage Industry" (https://medicareadvocacy.org/cms-caves-to-medicare-advantage-industry/).
