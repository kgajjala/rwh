Chipotle reported the third quarter on October 28 after the close and the stock rose 8.9% the next day to $31.28, then $31.89 on Friday. That is 16% above the $27.52 close of October 23 and about 14% under the September high. The move says the market expected something worse than traffic growth of 0.8% and a comp of 2.1%. The margin missed my bar, though, and that is the result that cuts value.

The traffic leg did what I asked. Transactions rose 0.8% against a 0.8% decline a year earlier, so the two-year stack went from −3.9% in Q2 to about zero, and comps beat the guide even with a Cyclospora drag that the company put at about 200 bps in July. CFO Adam Rymer said momentum slowed from mid-July and returned by late August, and CEO Scott Boatwright said September comps were up 3.8% with little promotion. Digital reached 38.8% of sales and loyalty a record 33% of sales.

The margin leg did not. Restaurant margin fell 120 bps to 23.3%: food cost improved 50 bps on price and tariff relief, labor rose 50 bps on volume, wages and promotional staffing, and other operating costs rose 100 bps on marketing, delivery mix and utilities. The fourth quarter is guided lower, and Rymer was direct about it:

> "Altogether, this will put Q4 margins at just under 22%, which is roughly 150 basis points lower than last year."
> — Adam Rymer, CFO, Q3 2026 earnings call, October 28, 2026

Half of that gap is a prior-year gift-card benefit and about 30 bps is the Smoked Brisket marketing push, so the underlying drop is smaller. But the quarter's cost lines show that price at about 2.7% in Q3 and 3% in Q4 is only matching inflation, which the CFO said runs low to mid single digits again in 2027. Q4 comps are guided to about +2%, with an exit rate of 3–4% that depends on Brisket. October "softened, consistent with the broader industry," in Rymer's words, though Boatwright said transactions have turned positive again.

David Palmer of Evercore asked the question the market had been asking, which is whether October was down, and he did not get a number. I read Q4 comps of +2% with about 3% of price as transactions near +0.5%, which is under the +1.5% my first test requires. I expect that leg to fail.

**Scorecard.** The first test (turn, not compare) is half resolved: its Q3 leg passed and its Q4 leg is pending. The second test (price catches beef) failed on the number, 23.3% against a 24.0% bar, and the fourth-quarter guide adds to it, so I apply its consequence as written: the Base restaurant margin is capped at 24% and Base value falls. The model's cap gives [[Base.value]] against the $44 I pre-committed, the difference being the lower base year and fewer shares. The third test (AUV at or above $3.10M) is pending to February; $3.106M passes for now.

Two things happened and they point the same way. My value fell 3% on the margin cap. The price rose 16% on a print that beat on traffic. The verdict is unchanged (Watch for a non-holder, Hold for a holder), so no bias check is needed, and the change is price and evidence in about equal parts.

**Buyback.** Chipotle bought back $110 million in Q3 at $34.05 because a blackout kept it out of the market for part of the quarter, and Rymer said it will return after the blackout. Year to date it has spent $1.4 billion at an average $34.32 with about $1.6 billion authorized. Cash and investments are $1.0 billion, up from $0.78 billion in June, and there is no debt.

**Appearances.** The Q3 call is the only management appearance I found between October 23 and today. Insiders: no filings since the controller's August sale. Short interest is 3.05% of float.
