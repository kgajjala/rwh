## Business Overview

Robinhood Markets, Inc. (NASDAQ: HOOD) is a U.S. retail brokerage and financial-services platform. Founded 2013 by Vlad Tenev and Baiju Bhatt with the insight that *commission-free* mobile-first trading would unlock the under-40 retail demographic, Robinhood IPO'd July 2021, survived a near-existential 2022–2023 crypto/retail-trading bear market, and has emerged as a structurally profitable multi-revenue platform.

**Scale (Q2 2026 / FY2025)**:
- 28.4M Funded Customers (+7% YoY)
- $369B Total Platform Assets (+32% YoY)
- 4.8M Robinhood Gold subscribers (+39% YoY; ≈17% of funded base)
- $21.7B Q2 net deposits (28% annualized growth rate; record)
- ARPU: $187 Q2 2026 (+24% YoY)
- Cash: $5.0B+; $2.2B zero-coupon convertible (June 2026); $664M YTD buybacks

**Five-stream revenue model (Q2 2026)**:
- **Options PFOF (≈26%)** — $342M, +29% YoY
- **Net Interest Revenue (≈30%)** — $389M, +9% YoY (margin lending + sweep + securities lending)
- **Event Contracts / Prediction Markets (≈12%)** — $156M, +>10× YoY (Rothera)
- **Equities PFOF (≈10%)** — $129M, +95% YoY
- **Crypto (≈8%)** — $100M, –38% YoY (2nd consecutive quarterly decline)
- **Gold + Other (≈11%)** — $143M, +54% YoY (incl. Trump Account service fees)

**Defining 2025–2026 transformation**:
- **Robinhood Chain mainnet (July 1, 2026)** — L2 Ethereum (Arbitrum); stock tokens in 120+ countries; Robinhood Earn 7% APY; 100M+ transactions, $12B+ DEX volume by Q2 call
- **WonderFi closed (June 1, 2026, ≈C$250M)** — Canada launched July 1
- **$2.2B zero-coupon convertible notes (June 2026)** — due 2029; conversion at $300 (non-dilutive unless >$300)
- **~290 employee layoff (June 16, 2026)** — ~10% of workforce; "talent density" restructuring
- **CTO Jeffrey Pinner departed (May 7, 2026)** — unplanned; no successor named
- **Trump Accounts** — 7M of 60M eligible children signed up; $1.5B in contributions; revenue recently began
- Prior: TradePMR (Feb 2025), Bitstamp (June 2025), S&P 500 inclusion (Sept 2025)

CEO Vlad Tenev (co-founder, 51 sells / 0 buys over 5 years; CEO since founding); CFO Shiv Verma (succeeded Jason Warnick mid-2025).

## Pivotal Investment Question

> Can Robinhood's *non-crypto* franchise — Gold subscriptions, net interest revenue, event contracts/Rothera, retirement, RIA custody (TradePMR), and international (Bitstamp + WonderFi/Canada + Singapore) — durably absorb the cyclical drawdowns in crypto transaction revenue so that the company compounds adj EBITDA through-cycle?

**Q2 2026 provided the strongest answer yet**: total revenue +32% while crypto –38% for the second consecutive quarter. Non-crypto franchise generated $1.21B of $1.31B total revenue — 92% of total. The answer is trending decisively toward *yes*, though a full crypto bear cycle (2022-style) has not been re-tested.

## Section 3 — Competitive Moat & Landscape

### Moat Verdict: **Narrow-and-Widening**

Robinhood does not yet have SCHW's scale moat ($11.8T AUM vs. HOOD $369B), but it has built a *cohort moat* (under-40 active trader; Gen Z 4× over-indexed) and is building durability layers. Robinhood Chain adds a *protocol moat* — first major U.S. brokerage to operate its own blockchain.

**Sources of moat**:
1. **Brand + UX with the under-40 cohort** — 4.8M Gold subs at +39% YoY suggests pricing-power emergence ($75/yr)
2. **Active-trader feature lead** — options + futures + 24/5 trading + Robinhood Legend desktop
3. **Vertical integration in event contracts (Rothera)** — owns the exchange; competitors don't have integrated brokerage distribution
4. **Bitstamp infrastructure** — 50+ global crypto licenses; hard to replicate
5. **Robinhood Chain** — own L2 with stock tokens in 120+ countries; 100M+ transactions in first month
6. **Direct-deposit / banking attach** — 40% of Gold customers direct deposit

**Vulnerabilities**: No scale moat vs. SCHW/Fidelity/Vanguard; crypto revenue cyclicality; PFOF regulatory tail; customer concentration in active traders; Tenev insider selling pattern

| Peer | Market Position | Moat / Threat to HOOD |
