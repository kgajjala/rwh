## Verdict

**America's lowest-cost custodian is now printing record returns on a capital base that barely grows, but after a 19% rally the probability-weighted case pays 12.4% a year. That is just under the hurdle, so the entry to wait for is at or below $98.**

**Breaks if**: clients start moving sweep cash out again as the Fed hikes, the 2022–23 cash-sorting pattern. Q2 added $24.2B of sweep cash. A quarter of net outflows with rates rising would mean the path to a 3.25%+ NIM is borrowed, not earned.

## The Call

The market is treating Schwab as a rate trade. On Sep 22 the stock fell **6.1%**, six days after the Fed's first hike since 2023 and on the day Altruist launched an AI planning agent. But Schwab's own Q2 10-Q, the first on a dynamically sized balance sheet, shows **+100 bps of rates adds 3.6% to net interest revenue** and −100 bps costs only 1.6%. The hedging that followed the 2023 scare has made the income statement far less rate-sensitive than the price action assumes.

What the market underweights is the capital math. Between FY2022 and FY2025 net income to common rose **$1.8B** on only **$4.1B** of added common equity (ex-AOCI), a **~43% incremental return**, and Schwab returned more than it earned in FY2025. Growth comes from asset gathering: $120B of core net new assets in Q2 (+49%), none of which needs retained capital.

What keeps this a Watch is the multiple arithmetic, not the business. The Base case compounds at **~11% a year at a flat multiple**, so the full 13% needs a small re-rating on top. The quality is not in question. The price stopped being generous at about $98.

## What I'd Have To Be Wrong About

1. **That higher rates raise NII instead of restarting cash sorting.** Test: transactional sweep cash in the **Oct 15** Q3 release and the October–November monthly reports. A quarterly decline of more than ~$15B while the Fed is hiking is the 2022 pattern returning.
2. **That the earnings step-up survives peak NIM.** The Base needs FY2027 near $7.60 and then ~8% a year. Test: the FY2027 scenario at the **January 2027 Winter Business Update**. Guided EPS growth below ~10%, or a 4Q26 NIM outside 3.25–3.30%, puts the Base under review.
3. **That AI agents do not commoditize advice or custody.** Test: Advisor Services organic growth and total core NNA stay at or above a 5% annualized rate through the Q4 print (Jan 2027). A slide below 4% would mean Altruist-style agents are pulling assets from the custody franchise, not just headlines.

## 4. Management & Capital

**Rick Wurster** (CEO since Jan 2025) and **Mike Verdeschi** (CFO) have now run two record years. Founder **Charles Schwab** and **Walt Bettinger** are co-chairmen.

**Incentives** ([2026 proxy](../../../raw/SCHW/filings/SCHW-DEF14A-2026.txt)): the annual cash bonus is **100% adjusted diluted EPS against plan** (FY2025 target $4.18, actual $4.87, paid at 116.5%). PBRSUs pay on **three-year average ROTCE divided by cost of equity** (the 2023–25 award paid 100% on 20.3% vs. a 9.5% COE). The return metric is the right one. The EPS target, set well below outcome, is soft. **Guidance grade: Consistent beater**: the 2026 revenue scenario has been raised twice (January → +14–15% in May → +17.5–18.5% in July).
